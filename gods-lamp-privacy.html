God's Lamp
Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

God's Lamp is a music creation app designed to work locally on your device. The Android release covered by this notice does not require an Unfire account and does not include the Android Internet permission.

1. Operator and contact

God's Lamp is offered by Omar Baró, operating the independent Unfire project from Spain. Unfire is not represented here as a separately incorporated company. For app support and privacy questions, email hello@unfire.technology.

2. Camera and gesture control

If you choose to use camera-based gesture controls and grant the required permission, God's Lamp uses your device camera to interpret hand movements. The app handles these images locally; it does not send camera images to Unfire servers. You can deny or revoke camera access in Android settings. Features that do not depend on the camera remain available.

3. Microphone and recordings

If you grant microphone permission and start a recording, the app uses microphone audio for your music session. Audio processing occurs on your device. Recordings and files that you choose to keep may be stored in the app's local storage or at a location you select through Android's file system. They are not uploaded to Unfire servers by this Android release. You may manage or remove local files using the app's available controls or your device's file and app settings.

4. Music projects and sharing

Music settings, performances and other session data may be stored locally to support app functions. If you export or share a file using an Android system interface or another application, that destination may process the file under its own privacy terms. You decide whether to initiate such sharing. Device backup and file-sync services that you separately enable are controlled by their respective providers.

5. Accounts, advertising and analytics

This Android release does not require an Unfire account, does not include in-app advertising, and does not use behavioral tracking or an app-operated analytics service. The application itself does not connect to Unfire servers to collect personal information, recordings, camera images or audio. Google Play, Android and your device manufacturer may process distribution, security, device or diagnostic information independently under their own policies.

6. Retention and your choices

Unfire does not maintain a server-side user profile or music-file archive for God's Lamp. Music and recording files saved locally remain under your device's storage and deletion controls. You can revoke camera and microphone permissions in Android settings. To remove the app's local data, use Android's app storage controls, bearing in mind that this may delete music files stored within the app.

7. Contact messages

If you write to hello@unfire.technology, the email service used by Unfire processes the message and your reply address so we can respond. This correspondence is separate from normal use of God's Lamp. You may ask about access, correction or deletion of such correspondence by email, subject to applicable legal requirements.

8. Changes

If the app's data practices change, this notice will be updated and the revision date changed. This policy describes the Android release and does not automatically describe a future iOS version with different permissions or services.
