THE FACTORY

All of UNFIRE in one app. Every machine connects to the others: Studio, Synths, DJ, Player and Camera share tempo, key and patch cables.

Tip: use headphones when recording vocals. The microphone and camera only activate when you choose to turn them on.

GESTURE CAMERA

Control parameters using your hands or movement. In low light, night vision enhances darker areas. Bright green or neon-pink gloves can help improve tracking.

UNFIRE DRAGON · KEY RESONATOR

Turn a source signal—vocals, percussion or a field recording—into harmonies aligned with your beat’s key. The dragon aims to keep generated pitches within the selected scale.

The current key appears in the top-left corner. Change the Studio key and the Dragon follows it. Camera gestures can also control it through PATCH.

PATCH · CONNECT EVERYTHING

Connect supported sources—hand gestures, camera zones, LFOs, kick detection and other compatible Unfire modules—to adjustable parameters across your devices.
