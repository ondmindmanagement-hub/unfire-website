# Unfire Audio — Real recorded effects (2026-10-27)

All 15 MP3s in this directory are edited copies of real-world recordings, not AI-generated sound and not film/movie SFX. Filtering, trimming, crossfading and MP3 conversion were used; original recordings have not been modified.
