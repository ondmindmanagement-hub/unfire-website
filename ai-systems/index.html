Ten engineering principles

Every Unfire system is designed against the same ten checks. They describe how we build, not a certification.

  1. TruthNo claim without evidence. Status is shown as it is.
  2. SafetyExplicit permissions, hard limits and a STOP that always works.
  3. PrivacyCollect the minimum. Secrets never leave where they belong.
  4. ContinuityWork is preserved and every state can be recovered.
  5. DeduplicationNo repeated actions, no duplicate processes, no replays.
  6. PerformanceCareful with CPU, memory, disk and network.
  7. BeautyClear, calm interfaces that are a pleasure to use.
  8. VerificationReal tests, regression checks and kept evidence.
  9. ReversibilityIsolated changes with a path back.
  10. AutonomyTake the next safe step — within the limits a person set.

No hidden connections

This website does not connect to your Mac, your terminal or any AI agent. Browser tools listed here run on your device. Any future remote connection will only be offered after authentication, permission and security testing — and it will say so clearly.
