02 / Working method

Three decisions before delivery.

01 — DISCOVER

Define the problem.

Identify a buyer-side process owner, budget authority, a recurring business impact and clear limits. No customer records or credentials needed at first contact.

02 — VALIDATE

Test what is feasible.

Confirm the actual software, staffing, data rights, privacy, security, technical dependencies and lawful contracting route before describing a delivery.

03 — AGREE

Accept written milestones.

Only mutually approved scope, documented acceptance tests, payment schedule and support terms create an engagement. Exploration alone does not.

03 / Private enquiry

Start with the decision you need to make.

Share a non-confidential outline of one business problem and what a useful outcome would be. Your answers are composed into an email locally on your device; Unfire receives nothing unless you choose to send it yourself.

No calls, mandatory travel, registrations, charges or access requests are created by this page. No proposal is binding. Contracting depends on eligibility, technical capacity, intellectual-property rights, taxes and written terms.
