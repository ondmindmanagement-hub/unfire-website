Commercial formats

The right model for the use case.

We are open to partnerships, but an approach is only commercially available after feasibility, ownership, third-party dependencies and eligibility have been reviewed.

01 / PROOF OF CONCEPT

Bounded paid pilot

A single measurable challenge, non-sensitive or synthetic inputs, agreed acceptance tests, a demonstrator and a written report. No unapproved production access.

02 / ENGINEERING

Integration or bespoke build

Adapt an interaction, workflow or visual concept to a customer's existing environment. New platform work is specified and tested separately; not assumed ready-made.

03 / INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

Licensing and OEM discussion

Explore non-exclusive licensing, white-label adaptations or limited component reuse, subject to provenance, dependency licenses, scope, maintenance terms and written agreement.

From demo to evidence

Prove a narrow claim first.

We prefer small experiments with baselines and repeatable results over broad performance promises.

Hands-free interaction

Compare pointer actions, click reliability, false activations and task completion to a standard input baseline in consenting, controlled tests.

AI workflow oversight

Measure approvals, blocked invalid actions, missed exceptions, audit record completeness and recovery on fictional workflows.

WebGL and procedural scenes

Deliver a small interactive browser experience and measure load time, frame pacing, device compatibility and accessibility.

Portability and integration

Start with a capability/dependency assessment, then a small reproducible build or API adapter with an explicit pass/fail target.
