02 · Our approach

Evidence before acceleration claims.

Performance is not one number. A viable workflow has to remain correct, safe and predictable under the same conditions as the baseline.

Define a baseline

Choose one non-sensitive task. Fix the device, software versions, model, inputs and acceptance criteria.

Test a bounded change

Evaluate reversible improvements such as repeated-work elimination or reduced workflow overhead, with consent and human oversight.

Report every outcome

Compare latency, reliability, task quality, memory, calls and costs where measurable. Include failures and negative controls.

For engineering teams
