Practical details

Commercial clarity matters.

Can our own brand and products appear in the 3D case?

Yes, subject to verifying the rights to the supplied assets. Branding, source-code access, white-label terms and any licence are agreed per project.

Can another agency subcontract this work to Unfire?

We are open to contracted agency delivery, including confidential or white-label work. Confidentiality, intellectual-property rights, acceptance criteria and support must be documented.

Will a complex 3D experience run on every device?

We cannot promise universal performance. We define target browsers and devices, performance budgets, useful fallback experiences and checks before a production commitment.

Does a shared multi-brand shopping centre exist already?

The shared commercial venue is a proposed initiative, not a currently verified hosted marketplace. The live Unfire game is a separate demonstration.

Must we pay an ongoing subscription?

No. Retainers, maintenance, distribution fees and technology licences are optional, scoped and contracted separately. A bespoke project can also be standalone.
