Pilot process

Simple. Remote. Respectful.

01 / CONTACT

Tell us your use case.

Email the product, device, country, intended use and what you'd like to test. Avoid sharing medical records, secrets or confidential project files.

02 / ELIGIBILITY

We verify compatibility.

We confirm whether an official App Store version, TestFlight build or properly signed and notarized Mac pilot is actually available. We never send unverified installers.

03 / FEEDBACK

Try it and tell us.

Any feedback is optional and can be critical. We do not buy, reward, require or selectively solicit positive ratings or reviews.

About early access

We aim to offer no-cost evaluation opportunities when eligible and technically available. This page is not a claim that every product, platform or trial is currently free or downloadable. Storefront prices, legal terms and platform eligibility must be checked before installing. An invitation does not guarantee access.

Participation is by written correspondence. We do not require calls or in-person meetings. We ask only for information needed to determine pilot suitability, and we do not request passwords, payment cards or access to sensitive personal data. Commercial engagements, if any, require separate written terms.
