Unfire Signals
Privacy Notice

Product version 1.4 · Drafted 20 November 2026 · Applies to the Unfire Signals browser extension, not other Unfire products

Unfire Signals is a browser extension being developed to show whether ChatGPT appears to be responding, finished or idle. This notice describes the implementation of the 1.4 release candidate. If a later released version changes how data is processed, this notice will be updated.

1. Operator and contact

The operator is Omar Baró, based in Spain, operating under the independent initiative name Unfire. Unfire is not currently a separately incorporated company. Privacy enquiries: hello@unfire.technology. See the legal notice.

2. Data and processing

With monitoring enabled and the required site permission granted, the extension examines the structure of permitted ChatGPT pages to identify reply activity. To notice changes, it may read visible assistant-response text and derive short, transient numerical fingerprints. It does not retain the complete message body or send it to Unfire servers.

The browser grants a tab API that can enumerate tabs; the extension limits its status inspection and displayed tab entries to chatgpt.com and chat.openai.com. The local dashboard displays matching tab titles, their approximate status and window information. Tab titles may themselves contain details chosen by the user.

3. Storage, transfers and retention

The extension stores an enabled/disabled preference in browser-local storage. Reply states, fingerprints and tab identifiers are used in temporary extension memory and may be discarded on browser or extension restart; turning monitoring off clears cached tab-state entries. The extension does not include an Unfire account system, analytics SDK, remote tracking endpoint, or message-body upload.

ChatGPT continues to communicate with its own service independently of Signals, under that service's terms. Browser and operating-system providers may process diagnostic information outside the extension's control. Opening this website also involves hosting and network requests subject to the general Unfire website privacy notice.

4. Permissions

  • Tabs: identify open ChatGPT tabs, display their titles and switch to the selected tab.
  • Scripting: detect response status on the two permitted ChatGPT domains; legacy browser builds may use the equivalent tab-script mechanism.
  • Local storage: remember the monitoring preference.

Other websites are not intentionally inspected by the extension. The extension does not request account passwords, contact lists, payment details, or access to unrelated sites.

5. Controls and rights

You can pause monitoring from the popup, revoke the extension's site permissions, remove the extension or clear browser storage. If you contact us, we will handle your communication as described in the general privacy policy. Under applicable data protection law, you may have rights to access, rectify, erase or restrict personal data and object to certain processing. Contact hello@unfire.technology. You may complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

6. Changes

This statement applies to a release candidate and is subject to verification against the exact submitted store build. Material changes in data use or supported services will require an updated notice before distribution.
