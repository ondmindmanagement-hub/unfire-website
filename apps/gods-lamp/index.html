What the privacy documentation describes

Ideas from gesture and sound.

These descriptions are based on the existing platform-specific product privacy notices. They are not a promise that both editions have identical features or that any store version is currently available.

01 / CAPTURE

Work with audio

Microphone access may be requested for optional recordings. Processing is designed around local device workflows.

02 / INPUT

Explore movement

The Android privacy notice describes camera-based gesture input. The iOS notice describes motion-related interaction.

03 / FILES

Keep creative control

Projects and music files are handled locally. Deliberate sharing or export follows the platform's available workflows.

Separate products and notices

The right information for each device.

Android

As of 21 November 2026, God's Lamp 1.0.0 (1) is in an active Google Play closed-testing track. Production access and public availability are not confirmed. Camera-based gestures, optional microphone recording and local music work are described in the platform-specific notice.

iPhone & iPad

An iOS 1.0.3 application archive was successfully uploaded to Apple's processing service on 21 November 2026. This does not establish review approval, release, availability or feature parity with Android. Audio, microphone, motion and local file handling are described in the iOS notice.
