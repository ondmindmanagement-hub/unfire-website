A recorder and sample editor for iPhone and iPad. Catch a sound, cut the exact part you want and export a clean WAV file.

Free on the App Store iPhone & iPad  ·  No account  ·  No data collected
01 / CAPTURE

Press record.

Use the device microphone and keep every take in the app’s local library.

02 / TRIM

Find the part.

Set start and end points on the real waveform and add optional anti-click fades.

03 / EXPORT

Take it anywhere.

Preview the selection, then export WAV to Files or another compatible app.

Does it upload my audio?

No. Recording and editing are performed locally. You decide when and where to share an exported file.

Which file do I get?

A standard WAV file. Choose 16-bit, 24-bit or 32-bit float depending on where it is going next.
