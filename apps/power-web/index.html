ABOUT THIS TOOL

Useful numbers.
No hidden assumptions.

Not a live electricity feed. Example values are fictional. Manual prices are supplied by you; imported REData JSON is processed entirely inside your browser and displays its own date and source. Always confirm the original publication and licence before further use.

PVPC is not the wholesale spot price. We accept only official indicator 1001 in the JSON import and convert its €/MWh values into €/kWh. Your contracted price may be different, particularly on a free-market tariff.
