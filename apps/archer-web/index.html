About this web experiment

Archer Web Lab uses its own Web Audio soft-clipping algorithm. It is not the native Archer DSP or an AU/VST plugin, and results may differ from the desktop app. Export uses 16-bit PCM; clipping may be audible at aggressive settings. We recommend auditioning at a comfortable volume. No backend or third-party audio service is used.
