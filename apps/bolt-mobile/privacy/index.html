BOLT Mobile — Privacy

Last updated: 13 October 2026 · English

BOLT Mobile is an offline, user-controlled review prototype for iPhone and iPad. This notice describes the current 0.2.0 development build, not the separate BOLT macOS product or unimplemented future features.

Files and review data

If you explicitly choose a UTF-8 text file through the system file picker, the app reads the selected file on the device to prepare a local proposed copy. The app does not silently scan other files or modify the original. You may choose to export a separate copy. The demo approval queue uses invented examples and is not connected to real workflows.

Data collection and network access

The current mobile build has no Unfire user account, advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK, hosted backend, remote agent or automatic file-upload feature. The app does not send imported text, review decisions or exported file contents to Unfire servers.

Local storage and retention

Selected or exported files remain subject to the storage location and backup settings you choose in iOS or iPadOS. Synthetic demonstration decisions are maintained within the app and are not a permanent enterprise audit log. You can manage locally exported files using the Files app and remove the app using your device settings.

Apple and other services

Apple may independently process App Store downloads, diagnostics and device information under Apple's own terms and privacy policies. If you email Unfire for help, the information in your email is processed separately as described in our general privacy notice.

Questions and rights

For access, rectification, deletion or other applicable data-protection requests involving messages you have sent to us, email hello@unfire.technology. Do not include confidential files or credentials in an initial support message.

This policy will be reviewed if functionality or data practices change before a public release.

Unfire is an independent technology initiative operated by Omar Baró in Spain; it is not a separately incorporated company. Contact: hello@unfire.technology.
