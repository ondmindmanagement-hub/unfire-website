Interfaces for

A civilian, research-stage exploration of gesture, voice and supervised software workflows where keyboards, mice or manual interfaces may be inconvenient. The focus is reproducible laboratory-style tests, not aerospace certification.

Research questions

Test under constraints.

01 · Input

Hands-free interaction

Can a camera-based gesture or voice interface reliably operate a mock instrument panel in benign conditions, with measurable errors and accessible fallback?

02 · Oversight

Human-authorised action

Can an interface show clearly what it intends to do before a simulated high-impact action, and reliably stop when a reviewer denies it?

03 · Recovery

Graceful failure

How should a prototype behave when a camera is obstructed, a voice command is ambiguous, a device disconnects or an operation times out?

First protocol

A civilian desktop mock-up.

1. Synthetic task

Control a non-operational on-screen instrument dashboard with dummy values. No connection to spacecraft, machinery, robots or safety-critical controllers.

2. Baseline and environment

Compare mouse/keyboard with experimental gestures or voice under declared OS, camera, microphone, lighting, acoustic and accessibility conditions.

3. Metrics

Record task-completion rate, median/p95 action latency, accidental activation, abort success, calibration time and operator preference. Report failures and sample size.

4. Safety gate

Require visible confirmation for simulated consequential actions. Provide a conventional input fallback and immediate stop. Avoid collecting identifiable biometric recordings without proper consent and approvals.

5. Honest publication

Publish a reproducible protocol and synthetic results only when actually run and checked. No claims of agency approval, laboratory validation, flight readiness or suitability for human life-support systems.

Collaboration

Ask a narrow, testable question.

Universities, educators, accessibility researchers and civilian laboratories can discuss a no-obligation feasibility outline by email. Research ethics, data protection, authorship, IP, fees and resources require case-specific written agreement.
