Commercial process

Remote. Written. Controlled.

  1. Send a non-confidential brief: organisation, country, project owner, one task, desired outcome, approximate budget range and deadline if relevant.
  2. We qualify scope: check fit, supported technology, legal/tax readiness and whether real operational data can be excluded.
  3. Receive a written proposal: agreed deliverables, exclusions, duration estimate, milestones, project-specific pricing and applicable taxes. No published price constitutes an offer.
  4. Proceed only if agreed: a separate written contract, required registrations and permissions must be in place before paid services or data exchange.

Important: Unfire is a Spain-based independent founder-led initiative and is not a separately incorporated company. Paid work is conditional on applicable tax, social security, benefits and other legal requirements.

Confidentiality: do not send credentials, production data, patient information or confidential documents in the first email. There are no required calls, office visits, deposits or paid applications to request a quote.

Ask for a quote

Describe the outcome, not a buzzword.

Use the subject “Unfire Pilot Marketplace — P01/P02/P03/P04”. Include your non-confidential problem, decision owner, budget envelope, country and desired acceptance test. A reply is not guaranteed and no agreement is created by an enquiry.
