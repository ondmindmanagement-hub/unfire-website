Gestures & physical computing / Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon · Indie 1st place

ControlAnything

Objects become interfaces. The team demonstrates hand-controlled connections to real devices, including an OP-1 Field synthesizer, a DJI gimbal and browsing gestures.

Why it stands out
A simple physical action makes the product's benefit legible in seconds.
What Unfire can apply
For Vision Control, show one actual gesture, an observable target response and a measured failure or recovery path.

Music & human-computer interaction / Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon · Best Creative Build

Synthexis

Camera-based gestures change plugin controls in FL Studio, with the resulting performance recorded as automation. The finished interaction runs locally on a Mac.

Why it stands out
One visible movement produces a result that musicians immediately understand.
What Unfire can apply
For gesture-control and audio work, capture the real DAW parameter changing, with legible UI and evidence of latency and precision.

External thumbnail hosted by YouTube · Creator: Linus Baumberger

AI agent oversight & developer security / Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon · Indie 2nd place

Gatekeeper

Security checks inspect npm packages without running them; suspicious evidence and a protective installation guard are shown alongside AI-assisted development.

Why it stands out
A rejected or blocked path is more persuasive when the evidence can be inspected.
What Unfire can apply
For BOLT, show a real action proposal, deterministic review or denial, and the trace that explains that outcome. No implied live production protection.

Our presentation method

Show. Verify. Explain.

The standards below guide future Unfire presentation work. They are editorial design goals, not claims that every Unfire prototype already meets them.

01 / INSTANT CLARITY

Start with the result.

The first five seconds should reveal who a product helps, what changes and why it matters. Show the use case before a long logo sequence.

02 / AUTHENTIC EVIDENCE

Demonstrate the real build.

Capture real, reproducible actions and outcomes. Clearly label browser simulations, synthetic data, future capabilities and incomplete integrations.

03 / RELEASE QUALITY

Respect every detail.

Readable responsive framing, accessible contrast, accurate narration, rights-cleared sound, judge-specific rules and a complete independent quality check.
