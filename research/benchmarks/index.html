Our presentation method

Show. Verify. Explain.

The standards below guide future Unfire presentation work. They are editorial design goals, not claims that every Unfire prototype already meets them.

01 / INSTANT CLARITY

Start with the result.

The first five seconds should reveal who a product helps, what changes and why it matters. Show the use case before a long logo sequence.

02 / AUTHENTIC EVIDENCE

Demonstrate the real build.

Capture real, reproducible actions and outcomes. Clearly label browser simulations, synthetic data, future capabilities and incomplete integrations.

03 / RELEASE QUALITY

Respect every detail.

Readable responsive framing, accessible contrast, accurate narration, rights-cleared sound, judge-specific rules and a complete independent quality check.
