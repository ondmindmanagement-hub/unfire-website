Research areas

Questions worth testing.

These are engineering research directions, not claims of validated products or published breakthroughs. Each topic progresses only when its assumptions can be tested.

01 / AI SYSTEMS

Reliable agents & handover

Bounded tool execution, human approval, recoverable workflows, traceable decisions and safe transfer of work between cooperating systems.

Research direction · Evaluation required
02 / INFRASTRUCTURE

Runtime reliability

Concurrency, queues, backpressure, CPU and memory limits, disk budgets, logging, caching and failure recovery under constrained resources.

Research direction · Evaluation required
03 / INTERACTION

Human-computer interfaces

Gesture, gaze and voice input; calibration, precision, accessibility, latency and safeguards against unintended commands.

Research direction · Evaluation required
04 / AUDIO ENGINEERING

Sound & embedded systems

Digital signal processing, portable audio workflows, real-time performance and the engineering constraints of compact synthesis hardware.

Research direction · Evaluation required

Verification & security

Repeatable tests, adversarial evaluation, provenance, permission boundaries, audit evidence, safe rollback and meaningful quality thresholds.

From research to deployment

Synthetic-data pilots, measurable feasibility criteria, human oversight and the gap between a controlled prototype and production readiness.

Research direction · Evaluation required

Research method

Make the evidence inspectable.

A research note should make clear what was asked, how it was tested, what was observed and what remains unknown.

01Question

Define the problem, hypothesis, scope and measurable success criteria before testing.

02Method

Record the environment, inputs, baselines, versions, relevant constraints and repeatable procedure.

03Evidence

Separate observation from interpretation. Retain test results and appropriately shareable artifacts.

04Limitations

Disclose failed cases, uncertainty, external dependencies and what has not been independently verified.

05Decision

Continue, redesign, archive or prepare a controlled pilot based on the evidence, not a marketing target.
