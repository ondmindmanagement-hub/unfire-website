01 / THE METHOD

Understand the bottleneck before proposing technology.

We map the handoff, reconcile what information is needed, identify who can approve an action and agree on a pass/fail test. Only then do we determine whether a Unfire tool, a custom component or no automation at all is the right answer.

DISCOVER
Find the expensive handoff

Describe one recurring exception and its owner. Set a baseline for time, errors or delayed decisions.

DESIGN
Define data and permissions

Specify which fields must match, which actions need explicit review and how failures are reported.

TEST
Evaluate a small synthetic case

Run fictional examples and document pass/fail results, unclear cases, limits and next-step requirements.

02 / PROBLEMS WE CAN SCREEN

Use cases shaped by operational needs.

OPERATIONS

Incident intake and triage

Check whether incoming requests can be normalized, routed by priority and handed to a reviewer with a clear decision trail. A feasibility scenario, not a deployed ticketing integration.

BACK OFFICE

Document and status exceptions

Test detection of missing fields, inconsistent statuses and approval boundaries with fictional purchase, delivery or reporting records.

AI WORKFLOWS

Human decisions before automated actions

Explore allow/review/block decisions, visible limits, logging and safe stop behavior. Bolt-inspired prototypes are assessed according to the particular task.

INTERFACES AND DATA

Usability and clearer information

Explore whether a simpler interface, energy-data view or alternative input could reduce friction. Accessibility benefits require separate user-led evaluation and are not assumed.

Technology selection is situational. Bolt, Unfire Control, Power and other Unfire work are not automatically bundled or represented as validated enterprise integrations.
