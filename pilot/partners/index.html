What makes an introduction useful

  • Who is the buying organisation and process owner?
  • What single workflow needs improvement, and how is success measured?
  • Is there a named budget owner, approved budget range and decision date?
  • Can we begin with entirely fictional data and one bounded workflow?
  • Can qualification, contracting and acceptance all happen in writing?

Clear commercial boundaries

  • Any feasibility fee or pilot budget is non-binding until a scope, price, deliverables and acceptance criteria are agreed.
  • Unfire is currently an independent initiative, not a separately incorporated company. Applicable Spanish tax, Social Security and benefits obligations must be resolved before paid delivery.
  • No confidential customer information or access to operational systems is requested during introduction.
  • No membership fee, intermediary commission, deposit, equity commitment or in-person engagement is accepted by default.
