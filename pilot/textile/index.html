A bounded assessment

Five stages, documented entirely by email.

  1. 01Describe

    One recurring, non-critical administrative exception.

  2. 02Define

    Rule table, review owner, expected allow/hold/block outcomes.

  3. 03Simulate

    Invent representative cases; no real orders or private records.

  4. 04Inspect

    Compare actual fixture decisions with expected results and limitations.

  5. 05Report

    Written evidence pack and go/hold/no-go recommendation.

Evidence and limits

What we have actually tested.

13 / 13

Developer-run Python checks passed on 12 October 2026, including nine deliberate input-manipulation cases in an offline synthetic handoff test.

  • An invented 14-event logistics fixture was classified offline. Five selected cases entered an isolated copy of Bolt's actual Swift TaskWorkflow; the other nine were held.
  • Three scripted approvals and two scripted rejections behaved as expected inside the isolated test sandbox. No actual employee approval or customer data was involved.
  • Zero external business-system changes were made. This result is not a real textile connector, customer trial, audited security system, productivity improvement or ROI measurement.
