Make flexible energy use easier to schedule.

An experimental decision aid for Spanish households and small businesses. It uses a simple trained forecasting model to rank electricity-use windows, explains the trade-off, and leaves every decision with the person.

Research-only demonstration. All hourly prices and historical examples below are generated synthetically for reproducibility. They are NOT live Spanish tariffs, grid readings, or validated emissions data. This demo makes no claim of actual CO₂ reduction, customer adoption, or appliance integration.

Method & safeguards. An offline deterministic sample-data generator creates repeatable price curves. A k-nearest-neighbours regressor estimates each future hour from historical hour-of-day patterns. A transparent contiguous-window search uses model estimates to find a cheaper feasible start time. A real 90-day pilot would add authorised historical price feeds, time-aligned grid emissions measurements, uncertainty calibration and opt-in household feedback; no emissions proxy is used in this demo. Incorrect advice is possible. Always verify real tariffs and constraints before changing consumption. The Power Energy Prices iOS application is a separate product; this research demonstrator does not claim to implement autonomous appliance control. Unfire.
