Three dials.
Nothing else.

ARCHER is a real-time clipper for producers, musicians and mix engineers who would rather listen than scroll through parameters.

GAIN

Drive.

Sets the input level before the clipping stage. Push it to decide how hard the signal hits.

THRESHOLD

Ceiling.

Sets where clipping begins, so you choose exactly how far the peaks are allowed to go.

ATMOSPHERE

Character.

Shapes the colour of the clipping, from discreet to clearly audible, without extra menus.
