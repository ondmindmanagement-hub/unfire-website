Short editorial description

ARCHER by UNFAIR is a compact macOS clipping tool for music producers and mix engineers. Its three-control interface offers direct access to gain, threshold and clipping character. ARCHER launched on the Mac App Store on October 2, 2026 and is listed at $9.99 in the United States.
