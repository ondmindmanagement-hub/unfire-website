About this demonstration

What Dante can answer

This first version looks up reviewed public Unfire information about current product pages, official contact routes and early-access research enquiries. Replies link to the underlying public page when available. Dante never claims to know a fact not present in the approved demo material.

What this version cannot do

This is a guided, rule-based preview rather than a general-purpose AI chatbot or a live connection to ChatGPT. It cannot run apps, control your computer, access private systems, inspect screens or respond reliably to questions outside its public knowledge base. The independent native application and richer conversational experience are separate development work.

Privacy and how it works

Questions are matched against a small public knowledge base locally within your browser. The Dante demo does not send message content to an AI provider or Unfire server, save chat history, set cookies, access a camera or use your microphone. If you enable spoken replies, the browser's speech-synthesis capability may be used; no microphone input is involved. Standard website hosting may still involve routine server access logs outside the Dante widget.

Demo terms and safe use

This preview provides general information only, with no guarantees of completeness or suitability for a particular use. Do not submit passwords, API keys, confidential business information, health information or other sensitive data. The demo does not perform actions or accept agreements on behalf of Unfire. Product availability, platform support and pricing must be checked on the linked official product pages.
