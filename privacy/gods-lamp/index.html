God's Lamp
Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026 · Applies to the Android app identified as technology.unfire.godslamp.

God's Lamp is a music creation app by Unfire, operated by Omar Baró. This notice explains how the Android app works with microphone audio, camera input and on-device music projects. It is separate from the Unfire website privacy policy.

1. Accounts, analytics and advertising

The current Android app does not require an Unfire user account and is not designed to collect or transmit personal information to an Unfire server. It does not include advertising, behavioral tracking or an in-app analytics service. Unfire does not sell users' personal data.

2. Camera and gesture controls

With your permission, camera access may be used to recognize hand gestures for musical controls. Camera images are processed on your device for this feature and are not sent to Unfire. You can stop using gesture controls or revoke camera access in Android settings.

3. Microphone and recordings

If you choose to record audio, the app requests microphone permission to capture sound for your music session. Audio processing and recording take place on your device. Recordings are not automatically uploaded to Unfire. Where supported, you control saving, exporting and deleting your work.

4. Local files and sharing

Instrument settings and music projects are handled on the device. If you deliberately export or share a file, the destination you choose may process that copy according to its own privacy practices. Android and any backup services configured separately on your device may also process files under their respective terms.

5. Store distribution and device services

Google Play manages app distribution, installation and any applicable store transactions under Google's own privacy practices. Unfire does not receive card-payment details through God's Lamp. Android manages permission prompts and may process device diagnostics according to your settings and Google's policies.

6. Support

If you email us for assistance, we receive the email address and information you voluntarily send. We and our email provider use that information to respond and manage the support request. This correspondence is separate from data handling within the God's Lamp app.

7. Your choices

You can manage camera and microphone permissions in Android settings. You can remove local app data by clearing its storage or uninstalling the app; files you exported, shared or backed up may need to be deleted separately. You do not need to create an Unfire account to use the Android app.

8. Changes and contact

If future releases change these practices, we will update this page and the corresponding Google Play disclosures. For questions or privacy requests, contact hello@unfire.technology.
