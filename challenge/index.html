Three tracks

Practical problems. Honest results.

01 · Human oversight

Explainable decisions

Demonstrate an action approval gate with allow, review, deny and a clear audit record. Use synthetic test data.

02 · Inclusive interfaces

Accessible interaction

Explore multimodal or alternative controls with fallback and consent. No clinical efficacy claims or unsupervised testing on vulnerable users.

03 · Reliability

Recoverable workflows

Show retries, failure handling, bounded resources or rollback with reproducible tests.

Participation

Small submissions. Clear evidence.

  1. Send an initial email: include your public project title, chosen track and non-confidential one-paragraph concept. No account, payment or private data required.
  2. Build independently: use your own tools or the Unfire reference example. No exclusive use of Unfire products is required. Keep your intellectual property.
  3. Prepare a reproducible demonstration: public link if you choose, install/run instructions, environment, test cases, failures, safety boundaries and a short results summary.
  4. Share only by permission: public showcasing, judging, dates and any recognition require a later confirmed programme and explicit contributor consent.

No money involved: entry is free; there are no cash prizes, deposits, paid registrations, guaranteed contracts or required subscriptions.

Recognition concept: an opt-in public showcase and demonstration roundup after review. There is no promised award or guaranteed selection.

Current gate: this page invites interest; it does not yet accept formal contest entries. Opening dates, eligibility and judging will be confirmed only after the participation terms and privacy process have been reviewed.
