Proposed participation framework

Who may participate

Proposed eligibility: adults aged 18 or over, including independent individuals and teams, where participation is permitted by local law. A definitive country eligibility and compliance review is required before any official contest opens.

Cost and reward

No entry fee, purchase requirement, subscription, cash or material prize. Any future recognition would be a voluntary public showcase, not a job offer, procurement promise or employment arrangement.

Ownership and publicity

Contributors retain ownership of their original work. Joining an interest list grants no intellectual-property transfer. We will not publish a contributor's name, code, video, logo or demo without explicit separate permission. Any public submission must respect third-party licences.

Evaluation proposal

Four equally weighted criteria: reproducibility, usefulness, safety/human control, and clarity of evidence. Final scoring, judging independence and conflict-of-interest rules will be published before accepting official entries. No winner has been selected or promised.

Safety and privacy

Do not send customer data, medical information, credentials, proprietary datasets or other sensitive materials. No production-system access, harmful automation, undisclosed surveillance or human-subject experiments are part of the challenge.

Updates, withdrawal and contact

Interest emails are non-binding and are not formal contest entries. Participants may request removal from an interest contact list by replying. Final contest dates, privacy handling, dispute procedures and eligibility terms are subject to legal and operational review before opening.
