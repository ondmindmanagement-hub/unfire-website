Autonomy Convergence and Control Vision

Unfire Autonomy Convergence is a research-stage direction for supervised workflow and information coordination, with traceable review points. A separate synthetic incident-review source and tests is publicly accessible; it does not establish a deployed platform or tested enterprise impact.

Unfire Control Vision explores camera-based gesture, gaze-related input and voice interaction on macOS. Precision, calibration, unintended activation and privacy are ongoing research constraints.
