AI · Automation · Computer workflows

Bolt

Bolt explores controlled AI orchestration for complex computer work. It is designed to coordinate AI systems, local tools and execution processes while preserving visibility, permissions and human control.

The project investigates how repetitive interaction between people, models and software can be reduced by allowing a validated plan to continue through execution, testing and repair cycles with fewer unnecessary interruptions.

Technology developed around Bolt can contribute to future automation, development and computer-control systems across the wider Unfire ecosystem.

Orchestration · Local execution · Controlled autonomy · Reusable automation
Energy · Data · Information systems

Power

Power explores how complex electricity and energy-market information can be converted into software that is easier to understand, compare and use.

The project combines regional data, pricing information and structured interpretation into a clearer interface, creating a foundation for future energy intelligence tools.

The same approach can later support other information-heavy products where fragmented external data needs to become understandable and actionable software.

Energy intelligence · Data interpretation · Information architecture
Internal infrastructure · Software production

UnfireSuite

UnfireSuite represents the internal production layer behind multiple Unfire projects.

Its purpose is to reduce repeated engineering by allowing software components, validation processes, production systems and accumulated technical knowledge to be reused where appropriate.

This is important because the value of one project does not need to end when that project is completed. Parts of the technology can become infrastructure for the next product, allowing the production system itself to improve over time.

Shared infrastructure · Validation · Reuse · Production systems
Unfire Audio · First implementation

Archer

Archer is the first product developed within the broader Unfire Audio direction.

It is a precision audio clipping tool built around Gain, Threshold and Atmosphere, designed to give producers and engineers direct control over clipping behaviour, peaks, transients and sonic character.

Its strategic role is broader than a single audio effect. Archer provides a practical environment for developing and validating audio technology, interface systems and production components that can later contribute to future creative-software products.

Unfire Audio is also exploring how AI-assisted systems, procedural processing and reusable software technology can converge into new plugin concepts and creative workflows beyond conventional fixed audio-processing tools.
