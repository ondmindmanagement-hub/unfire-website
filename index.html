Company thesis

Software products are the output. Compounding capability is the strategy.

Unfire is being built around a simple principle: knowledge, infrastructure and engineering work produced for one project should strengthen the projects that follow. Instead of treating every application as an isolated development effort, Unfire is developing a shared technical foundation that can reduce duplicated work, improve repeatability and accelerate validation across different software categories.

Business architecture

Several paths to revenue. One technology company.

Software

Paid applications, professional tools and specialised digital products across selected technology verticals.

Infrastructure

Shared technology and future platform services capable of supporting multiple products without rebuilding the same foundations repeatedly.

Value-linked services

Where appropriate, future business infrastructure can use management or success-fee models aligned with measurable economic value rather than relying only on fixed software licences.

Funding rationale

Funding accelerates the transition from technical validation to market validation.

External funding would not simply finance another isolated application. It would strengthen a reusable European software-development capability and help convert the company's existing technical progress into secure, tested and commercially validated products.

Product hardening

Independent QA, reliability testing, accessibility, security reviews, performance work and production-readiness.

Commercialisation

Distribution, market validation, documentation, customer support, internationalisation and launch infrastructure.

Core capability

Further development of reusable internal infrastructure while preserving the confidential know-how responsible for Unfire's production advantage.

Responsible reporting. Unfire does not present internal candidates as completed commercial releases and does not publish unverified acceleration percentages. Production advantages will be benchmarked using repeatable measures including development time, human intervention, cost, build reliability and time to deployable candidate.
