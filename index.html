AI · Automation · Computer workflows

Bolt

Bolt explores controlled AI execution for complex computer workflows. It connects AI systems, local tools and software actions while preserving permissions, visibility and human approval where needed.

The focus is practical: make multi-step work more traceable, measurable and controllable from instruction through execution.

Technology developed around Bolt can support future automation and software products across Unfire.

AI orchestration · Local execution · Permissions · Auditability
Energy · Data · Information systems

Power

Power turns electricity and energy-market data into software designed to make complex information easier to understand, compare and use.

The project brings regional data, pricing information and structured interpretation into a clearer interface for comparison and decision support.

The same data and interface principles can support future products built around complex external datasets.

Energy intelligence · Data interpretation · Information architecture
Internal infrastructure · Software production

Unfire Suite

Unfire Suite is the internal production layer supporting multiple Unfire projects.

It reduces duplicated engineering by reusing software components, validation processes, production systems and technical knowledge where appropriate.

Work completed for one product can strengthen the next by contributing proven components, processes and infrastructure.

Shared infrastructure · Validation · Reuse · Production systems
Unfire Audio · First implementation

Archer

Archer is the first product developed within Unfire Audio.

It is a precision audio clipping tool built around Gain, Threshold and Atmosphere, designed to give producers and engineers direct control over clipping behaviour, peaks, transients and sonic character.

Beyond the individual product, Archer provides a practical environment for validating audio technology, interface decisions and production components.

Unfire Audio also explores how AI-assisted systems, procedural processing and software can support new creative tools and workflows.
