Portable Synth - DAW
Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Portable Synth - DAW is designed to make music on your device. It does not require an Unfire account, and the Android build covered by this notice has no Internet permission, advertising SDK or in-app analytics.

1. Operator and contact

Portable Synth - DAW is operated by Omar Baró under the independent Unfire project in Spain. For questions about this app or privacy, contact hello@unfire.technology. Unfire is not represented here as a separately incorporated company.

2. Microphone

With your permission, the app can record audio using your device microphone when you explicitly start microphone recording. Audio is handled by the app and saved as project or recording files on your device. The app does not send recorded audio to Unfire servers. You can refuse or revoke microphone permission in Android Settings; synthesizer and keyboard features that do not use the microphone remain available.

3. Music projects, imported files and exports

Notes, audio tracks, imported sound files, recordings, editor settings and music projects are stored locally on your device or saved to a location you choose. When you select an Android file for import, the app receives access to that selected file through Android's file picker. Exporting or sharing a file through Android or another app is a separate action that you initiate; the receiving app or destination may have its own privacy practices.

4. Tilt / motion sensor

If enabled, the optional keyboard tilt-dynamics feature uses the device accelerometer to modify the attack intensity of notes. These readings are processed locally and are not uploaded. The app remembers your chosen sensitivity locally; it does not create an account or store a history of motion readings. You may disable tilt dynamics in the app.

5. Collection, sharing, advertising and analytics

This Android build does not directly transmit personal information, recordings, microphone audio, imported files, or accelerometer readings to Unfire. It does not contain an advertising service, behavioral tracking SDK or separate analytics SDK. There are no purchases or subscriptions in this free version.

Google Play, Android, your device manufacturer and the services you choose for downloading or sharing may process technical information such as app installation, diagnostics or usage data according to their own notices. Such platform processing is not described here as data received by Unfire.

6. Storage, deletion and security

Your music files and local settings remain on your device until deleted by you, removed with the application (subject to any separate files you exported or backed up), or managed by Android storage controls. You can remove saved projects and recordings using the relevant in-app features or your device's file management tools where available. The app does not provide cloud backup or a remote server account. Protect your device and exported files appropriately.

7. Support messages and legal rights

If you contact our support email, your email address and message are received by the email provider and used to reply and maintain related correspondence when necessary. The applicable lawful bases may include taking steps at your request, legitimate interests in support and compliance with legal obligations. Where relevant under the GDPR, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability, and you may complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD). Email requests to hello@unfire.technology.

8. Updates

This notice applies to the Android Portable Synth - DAW version described above. If future versions add network services, online collaboration, analytics or purchases, the notice and applicable disclosures must be reviewed and updated before those features are released. This policy is distinct from the Unfire website and browser-game policy.
