New: inspectable AI research evidence

Research update · 21 November 2026 · Public and reproducible within the stated limits

The BOLT Agent Control Plane is a distinct, MIT-licensed, read-only developer experiment. It takes structured AI action proposals and applies deterministic policy rules that classify them as allowed, requiring review or denied. It does not execute external actions, operate a live approval service, establish commercial production readiness or certify AI safety.
