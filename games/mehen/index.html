THE ORIGIN

Before kingdoms.
Before written rules.

Mehen was played in ancient Egypt more than five thousand years ago. Its coiled serpent board may have represented the protective god surrounding the sun. The surviving objects do not preserve the original rules.

Our version is a new, playable strategy game inspired by its shape, lion pieces and journey toward the center. It does not claim to reconstruct historical gameplay.

YOUR GUIDE TO THE SERPENT

How to play

These are modern Unfire rules, not verified ancient Egyptian rules.

  1. Each side has three lions. Gold begins.
  2. Cast the sticks to roll a number from 1 to 4.
  3. Select one of your highlighted lions to advance that number of spaces toward the center.
  4. Landing on a rival lion sends it back to the tail. Marked sanctuary spaces are safe from capture.
  5. Reach the heart by an exact throw. Overshooting is not allowed. The first player to finish two lions wins.
  6. If no move is possible, your turn passes automatically.
