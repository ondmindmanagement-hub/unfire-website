Unfire is an independent, founder-led software and creative technology initiative based in Spain. It is **not a separately incorporated company** at this stage.

## Technology and product areas

- [Bolt](https://unfire.technology/bolt/) — permission-aware AI workflow prototypes, human review and recovery controls.
- [Unfire Control](https://unfire.technology/apps/unfire-control/) — exploratory hands/voice interaction and accessibility-oriented interface research.
- [Archer](https://unfire.technology/archer/) — digital audio software.
- [Power](https://unfire.technology/apps/power/) — energy information tools.
- [Games and interactive experiences](https://unfire.technology/games/) — creative digital products.
- [Research](https://unfire.technology/research/) — research questions, methods and limits of evidence.

Check each linked product page for its actual release, test and validation status. Internal prototypes are not claims of certified or production-ready deployments.
