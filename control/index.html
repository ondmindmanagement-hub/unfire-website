Early-stage research · macOS

More ways to interact with your computer.

Unfire Control explores alternative computer interaction through camera-based movements and voice. We are seeking informed feedback to learn where these approaches may help people and workplaces.

Enquire about a research pilot Share individual feedback

No public download or paid access is offered on this page. Product availability, safety, accessibility performance and compatibility have not yet been independently validated.

Designed to be tested with people

One research programme, different use cases.

Individuals & accessibility

Explore whether alternative input could make everyday computer tasks easier for adults who find conventional mouse or keyboard interaction difficult. Every individual has different needs; research must account for those differences.

Employers & workplaces

Explore opt-in adaptations for macOS workstations, with IT and accessibility teams involved. We want to understand setup, privacy, existing assistive tools and potential integration constraints before discussing licensing.

Educators & researchers

Investigate small, controlled studies of gestures, voice, task success and unwanted activations. No student or patient data is needed for initial enquiries.

Developers & integrators

Discuss requirements for future APIs, configuration and custom command workflows. Integration and OEM licensing are possibilities for exploration, not shipping features.

Work with us

Request a written pilot conversation.

We welcome enquiries from disability-led organisations, independent adult testers, accessibility specialists, universities and employers. Initial discussions can take place entirely by email, without meetings, fees or commitment.

  • First: describe the general computer tasks you would like to explore. There is no need to disclose a diagnosis or other sensitive health information.
  • Then: we share a short written research protocol, feasibility limits and privacy/consent process for consideration.
  • Only after verified release readiness and informed agreement: consider an optional evaluation with clear ways to stop and return to conventional controls.
  • Any future enterprise pricing or agreements would be scoped and disclosed separately, not assumed from an enquiry.

Important current limitations

Research-stage prototype, not a certified assistive product. Do not rely on Unfire Control as the sole means of operating a computer, accessing essential services or performing safety-critical tasks. Features involving hand, head, gaze or voice interaction must be individually tested before claims of suitability. macOS permissions, signing, notarisation, stability, privacy and independent accessibility evaluations are release gates.

This page does not claim conformity with a particular accessibility standard or availability through an App Store. This is an independent technology initiative led by Omar Baró, not a claim that Unfire is an incorporated company.
