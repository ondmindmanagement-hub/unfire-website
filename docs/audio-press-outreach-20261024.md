## Pitch and workflow
A personal email of 75 to 120 words. One verifiable sentence for the recipient's channel or beat, one real differentiator, beta disclosure, direct game link, invitation to try free. Avoid promotional superlatives. No fee or requirement to cover.
No attachments until genuine assets are cleared. No bulk BCC, outreach automation spam, impersonation or requests for positive reviews.
First draft batch during weekend. Do not send until public build can be tested and local weekday daytime for recipient is confirmed.
Track recipient, verified source, channel angle, date sent, reply and one optional follow-up after a week. Stop on opt-out or no reply after one follow-up.
Do not publish this document's outreach list as proof that coverage was received.
