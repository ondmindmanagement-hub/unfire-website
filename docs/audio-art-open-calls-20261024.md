# Unfire Audio. Art, web culture and gallery outreach

Prepared 2026-10-24. Cost cap: EUR 0. No commissions, entry fees, paid features, hosting upgrades, donated ad credits requiring payment, in-person visits or calls.

## Curatorial framing
Working concept: Unfire Audio as an interactive dreamscape, not merely a game catalogue. Original robots, music and navigable 3D spaces work as experiential digital art. This is a curatorial interpretation and pitch, not a claim of institutional recognition.
Headline: "Unfire Audio."
Supporting line: "Step inside the dream."
Avoid the long typographic dash in marketing, in the app title and in email subjects.
Artist/creator attribution: "Unfire, independent creative project". Do not share any founder name or photo without separate approval.

## Free or potentially free channels verified from public pages
- CreativeApplications.Net, https://www.creativeapplications.net/contact/ . Art, media and technology project pitch via form. Prepare visual documentation of interactive behavior, not a product ad.
- C4CCI Art X Space, https://c4cci.com/open-call/ . Digital Encounters online exhibition call, deadline 30 Nov 2026, states submission fee free. Requirements: artist bio up to 200 words, artistic statement up to 300 words, 5-8 genuine works with metadata. Request clarification about collective/project attribution before submission; standalone commercial shopping layer may not fit the call.
- Async Museum, https://www.asyncmuseum.com/submit . Browser-native net art; excludes commercial projects. Do NOT submit the game as-is until a standalone non-commercial artwork meets their criteria and rights are fully cleared.
- THIRDPLAT "SOMETHING NEW", https://www.artconnect.com/opportunity/Ld3cJAs4qQyuRG1oujHi- . Worldwide online showcase, free application and participation, deadline 31 Oct 2026. Check direct platform's terms and artist identity requirements before any submission.
- Creative Boom, https://www.creativeboom.com/submit/ . Editorial submission is not a paid ad, but its visual arts section requires name, bio, location and six high-res 2600px images. Do not submit without approved identity and authentic media; may instead pitch its news desk on launch later.
- The Wrong Biennale, https://thewrong.org/ . Pavilion proposals cost money unless fee waiver granted. ONLY inquire about a full waiver if free process verified. Never sign or pay.
- Do not enter paid opportunities disguised as free applications with a mandatory post-selection exhibition fee.

## What an art packet needs
[ ] 5-8 approved actual screen captures or clips, each identified by scene and date.
[ ] 1600px or greater for galleries requiring large images; avoid upscaling tiny art without disclosure.
[ ] Brief curatorial statement, honest early-beta status.
[ ] Technical description (browser, WebGL, real-time interaction) verified by game owner.
[ ] Audio license review and confirmation of third-party media usage.
[ ] Author identification or project/collective identity that is accepted by organizer without misrepresentation.
[ ] Read rights clauses before signing; no exclusivity, no transfer of IP, no fees or travel.
[ ] No applications representing game as already museum-exhibited.

## Process
1. Hold during hardware QA for first-play experience.
2. Present press/art pages after integrator publishes.
3. Send small, tailored pitches to curators and editors; never mass unsolicited blasts.
4. Art exhibitions and forms requiring manual fields or artistic identity must be completed truthfully after requirements are confirmed.
5. Track acceptance, publication URL, external referral clicks and genuine play sessions only if privacy-respecting measurement is available.
