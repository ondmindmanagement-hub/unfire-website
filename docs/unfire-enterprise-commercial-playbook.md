# Unfire for Business — commercial playbook
Updated: 2026-10-23. **Internal strategy, not a customer-facing promise or a signed partnership.**

## Positioning
**Unfire makes products worth exploring—and gives companies a path from one experience to connected software.**
Sell the immediate, verifiable outcome first: a product demo or interactive catalogue with a clear purpose, owned rights, target devices, analytics agreed with the customer, and an email enquiry path. Then offer useful additions when value has been demonstrated.

Do **not** sell a hypothetical "virtual mall" as live or imply existing tenants or reach. The current public /audio/ experience is a game / technology demo, not a proven reseller platform. Build bespoke client worlds as separate packages, not by giving away the game's code, characters, media or trademarks automatically.

## Four revenue streams (opt-in, scoped separately)
1. **Custom interactive work:** fixed-scope 3D cases, product launches, immersive websites, agency subcontracting and white-label interactive content.
2. **Licences and maintenance:** optional ongoing software use, hosting operations, product updates, support and agreed service levels. Never assert "daily delivery" to a client unless the contract and capacity support it.
3. **Curated showcase network (future phase):** brand spaces, discovery events, premium placement or campaign sponsorship only after real traffic, sales infrastructure, editorial standards and explicit brand permission are available.
4. **Partner referrals / distribution:** commission on attributable and consented sales or a separately agreed promotion fee. Affiliate and sponsored content must be labelled. No affiliate links or third-party logos without permission and a real relationship.

## Ideal buyer and message
- **Manufacturers / retail brands:** "Present complicated products in an interactive 3D case that helps buyers understand options."
- **Agencies / production studios:** "White-label 3D engineering with written acceptance criteria and independent delivery."
- **Startups / software vendors:** "Turn a product demo into a connected experience, then pilot useful integrations."
- **Universities / exhibits / institutions:** "Interactive experiences subject to accessible fallback, content rights and clear scopes."
- **Reseller partners:** "Referral or placement only when traffic, attribution and truthful disclosure exist."

**Why Unfire?** Engineering, interactive presentations and experimentation exist under one initiative. A commissioned experience can be extended into permissioned integrations without redoing discovery from scratch. Cross-product interoperability is a possibility to evaluate, **not** a pre-existing universal connector or guaranteed outcome. Emphasise written milestones, responsive design, versioned releases, rights clearance and performance checks rather than unsupported global-scale claims.

## Commercial qualification (email-first)
Ask for the product category, catalogue size, target device/browser, desired interaction, available assets and legal usage rights, audience or geography, systems to integrate, accessibility requirements, procurement/fiscal requirements, deadlines and budget. Do not ask for passwords, API keys or sensitive customer data in the initial email.

Process: (1) brief -> (2) written feasibility and rights review -> (3) fixed-scope paid pilot with acceptance tests -> (4) signoff and delivery -> (5) optional maintenance/integration. No work starts without written scope, payment terms and appropriate legal/fiscal setup.

## Illustrative price hypotheses — NOT public offers
These are internal initial test ranges, not a tariff, quote, guaranteed income or proof of demand:
- Small branded case pilot: **€2,500–€7,500** one-off for a tightly specified scope, excluding unlicensed media and custom backend work.
- Bespoke digital showroom: **€8,000–€30,000+** depending on model creation, content, testing and integrations.
- Optional maintenance/licensing: **€250–€1,500+ per month** with bounded support, hosting costs and SLA defined; no indefinite unlimited revisions.
- Agency subcontracting: project fee and license structure negotiated; no unapproved transfer of IP.
- Referrals / paid placement: consider only after a partner programme, attribution, traffic evidence, brand approval, payment collection and legal review exist.

Verify rates against actual delivery cost, taxes, infrastructure, QA, customer acquisition, licences, privacy/security and margins. Income can be recurrent, but **not cost-free or guaranteed**.

## Scope, rights and legal gates
- **Current status:** founder-led, not yet incorporated. Verify the lawful contracting/invoicing route and tax implications before taking deposits or claiming an established company.
- **Intellectual property:** confirm right to reuse every model, music track, font, logo, plugin, library, API and third-party asset; build a bill of materials and licence register.
- **White-label:** separate retained core engine from customer-specific deliverables; contract ownership, permitted distribution, source escrow if any, maintenance and termination.
- **Brand access:** no partner brand or logo on-site without written approval; no implication of exclusivity, sponsorship, agency or endorsement.
- **Security/privacy:** GDPR notices, data processing agreement where applicable, no hidden analytics, secure integration permissions, opt-in telemetry, and written incident handling.
- **Advertising:** visibly label sponsored listings and affiliate links. Under Spain's LSSI, advertising must be identifiable and the advertiser clear. https://lssi.digital.gob.es/lssi/la-ley/aspectos-basicos/publicidad-en-internet
- **Sales:** no false scarcity ("last slot"), guaranteed growth, invented traffic, fabricated clients or association with third-party brands.
- **Performance:** define a test matrix and fallback UX for mobile/desktop. Do not guarantee 100% device compatibility, zero latency or uninterrupted operation.

## Pilot delivery acceptance checklist
[ ] Written brief and success measure
[ ] Rights register for assets and source dependencies
[ ] Security, personal-data and external integration review
[ ] Keyboard/touch control and reduced-motion checks
[ ] Tested target browsers/device sizes and rotation
[ ] Initial loading and sustained frame performance budget
[ ] Navigation, CTA, contact and analytics disclosure checked
[ ] Source/licensing/delivery/export rules and acceptance signed
[ ] Optional follow-on licence/maintenance separately agreed

## Outreach language (English, email-first)
Subject: Interactive product experiences built for your brand

"We are exploring tailored 3D product showcases and interactive digital experiences at Unfire. A focused pilot could turn one product line into a branded, browser-accessible experience, with agreed scope, rights and testing. If it demonstrates value, we can then discuss software integrations, licensing or continued support. We can review your requirements and send a written proposal by email."

No mass unsolicited campaigns by default. Check lawful contact permissions and offer an easy opt-out when marketing emails are permitted.

## Execution order and milestones
Phase 1 — sell one bounded, rights-cleared showcase from a demonstrable prototype.
Phase 2 — template and document reusable product-case components after successful QA and one paying reference customer (with permission).
Phase 3 — optional support and licensing contracts, test retention and unit economics.
Phase 4 — only when traffic and rights are verified, trial an explicitly sponsored, opt-in multi-brand destination.
Phase 5 — scale to partner network, agency channel and broader integrations.

The homepage currently has concurrent game-first and mobile/product work. Maintain this offering on isolated /enterprise/showrooms/ and merge only after green tests and a single owner validates navigation against the latest home-router changes.
