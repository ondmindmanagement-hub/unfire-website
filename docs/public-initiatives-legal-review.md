## Preliminary findings

1. **Challenge is interest only.** No formal competition registration, deadline, jury or winners exist. It offers no monetary prizes and no required purchase. Do **not** open formal entries, publish submitted code, identify participants, use sponsor logos or promise a winner before jurisdiction-specific rules, eligibility, cancellation, privacy and IP conditions have been approved.
2. **Email collection is not zero personal data.** `mailto:` opens a local email client but messages sent to Unfire may contain personal data. No newsletter or mailing list is activated by these pages. A complete Article 13 information notice with actual controller identity, purposes, lawful basis, contact details, retention or criteria, recipients/transfers and rights still requires the #140 owner. Do not invent retention periods or service-provider contracts.
3. **Spanish site identification:** the current #140 audit flags LSSI Article 10 transparency gaps in existing legal information. Do not put a private residential address, ID or confidential tax details in public GitHub without a lawful publication strategy and appropriate handling.
4. **Developer IP:** only `developers/examples/` has an express MIT licence. Existing Unfire source and the private Bolt/Control technology are not automatically licensed. The example is not a security product.
5. **Scientific claims:** no NASA, ESA, clinical, academic or external validation is asserted; controlled civil simulation only. Real human participants or sensitive data require protocols, consent and approvals.
6. **Commercial proposal:** all four offerings are non-binding project enquiries. No prices paid, contracts, government procurements, licences, safety/compliance certifications or ROI guarantees are claimed. Verify Spanish professional/tax/benefits status before first paid agreement.
7. **No third-party visual reuse:** the three inspiration image links supplied as design references were not used or embedded, and no external branding or logos were introduced.
