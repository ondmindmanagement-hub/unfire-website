# University and B2B qualification · No duplicate outreach

**Internal working framework; non-confidential summary only.** Do not add personal contacts, email threads or prospect secrets to public GitHub.

## Academic route triage

Use verified institutional challenge/capstone pages and responsible research groups. The existing Unfire contacts are already tracked in issue #50 and official mailbox; **do not recontact UPC/GESSI, RTU, UCL, UOC, YUFE, UPCT or other prior recipients without a concrete new reason and deduplication**.

Before sending any message, check:
- Does the programme accept **individual founder-led initiatives** based in Spain without company incorporation?
- Are there **zero required fees**, no institutional sponsorship, insurance or student-pay obligations?
- Can interactions be completed **remotely and asynchronously in writing**, without calls or on-site attendance?
- Can a faculty-led team study **synthetic benchmark data** rather than build Unfire's commercial product for free?
- Are IP, publication, supervision, data protection and any institution branding specified?
- Is the deadline **currently open** and the institution's contact/public submission route authentic?

This does not meet Unfire's no-calls condition without an authorised written exception. Source: https://stevens.capsource.app/program/fall-2026-stevens-industry-capstone-program/rfp

Source: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/genai4eu

## Three prioritised B2B buyer profiles (not specific confirmed leads)

1. **Software and AI development teams.** Pain: repeated builds, redundant prompts, opaque costs. Offer: measured one-workflow efficiency diagnosis.
2. **Audio plug-in and creative software studios.** Pain: expensive build-test cycles, audio regressions, device resource pressure. Offer: scoped repeated-build profile with correctness and audio QA gates.
3. **Research labs and SME technical teams.** Pain: AI experimentation overhead, unreliable execution and hard-to-audit workflows. Offer: synthetic pilot with latency, success, cost and recovery metrics.

Avoid regulated personal data, worker surveillance and replacing security controls.

## Outreach stages

1. Validate actual product capability and safe demo route.
2. Verify recipient decision authority and the published contact channel from an official source.
3. Search Unfire official mailbox (SENT, INBOX and DRAFTS); avoid duplicates and stale follow-ups.
4. Send a **specific, English-language, one-to-one** invitation only after qualifying.
5. Track status: identified, eligible, contact authorised, contacted, responded, proposed, contracted, declined. Do not label generic interest as a sale.
6. No paid subscriptions or commercial obligations before a separate approval and legal/fiscal check.
