# Legal / fiscal and privacy decisions required before ANY official edition

**Draft reviewer checklist, not legal advice.** Owner: #140, in coordination with #340 / PR #342 and the sole release integrator #78. The founder is an individual operating from Spain; do not misrepresent Unfire as incorporated or publish private home / tax IDs without an approved compliant disclosure plan.

## Spain / EU organizer review

1. Confirm which identity and address must be displayed by a Spanish founder under LSSI art. 10; identify a compliant **privacy-preserving public address** strategy and accurate mandatory business contact details. [BOE 34/2002](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2002-13758).
2. Confirm when organizing any prize competition (including in-kind or sponsored) constitutes a professional economic activity and whether any registration, fiscal status, insurance or special filing is required. Distinguish hosting recognition-only merit challenges from distributing money or equipment.
3. Distinguish skill-only, pre-declared jury competitions from lotteries/gambling and from *combinaciones aleatorias con fines publicitarios*; ensure no random tie-break, draw or paid-entry condition anywhere in the process. The [DGOJ FAQ](https://ordenacionjuego.es/preguntas-frecuentes?faq=194) is a starting point, not definitive legal clearance.
4. Determine Spanish and cross-border tax treatment of cash and in-kind prizes, value threshold if applicable, payer residency and possible reporting/withholding: do not automatically apply a single national percentage to every award.
5. Identify country-by-country permitted eligibility and delivery of prizes: Spain/EU/UK/USA and other requested jurisdictions **separately**. Add sanctions/export and age/parental-consent considerations as appropriate. “Global” indicates reach, not unlimited contest eligibility.
6. Approve versioned official rules with exact dates, objective scoring, tie review, disqualification, appeals, complaints, cancellation, unforeseen incidents, and compliant liability wording. Independent judges must accept and disclose conflicts before the first entry.

## Data and intellectual property

7. Provide accurate GDPR Article 13 notice for contestant interest and eventual entry: controller identity, purposes and lawful bases, retention criteria, rights channel, processors/cross-border transfers, data security, complaint authority. Do not guess deletion periods. [GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng), [AEPD guidance](https://www.aepd.es/derechos-y-deberes/conoce-tus-derechos/derecho-de-informacion).
8. Confirm platform and recipient agreements before transferring participants' work or details to a third-party competition platform; e.g. Kaggle makes certain competition submissions publicly visible after closing. Clear opt-in for future sponsor introductions or promotional name/logo/winner showcases. Competition entry must not be conditioned on marketing consent.
9. Confirm participant originality/third-party licenses and voice/likeness consent for the audio edition. No compulsory assignment of background IP and no undisclosed rights to train models with submissions.
10. Confirm secure intake and incident/appeal procedure: no public repo storage of applications, no uncontrolled uploads, no promise of GDPR compliance based only on static HTML.

## Awards, sponsors and independence

11. Verify physical/in-kind prize logistics, transferable donor/license authorization, VAT/customs/shipping, country exclusions and responsibility for unsuccessful delivery. Do not advertise donations without written sponsor permission.
12. Verify when cash awards can be promised: ring-fenced funding, accountable payer, lawful payment rails, withholding/reporting and tax statements; no private or business bank details in GitHub.
13. Confirm whether a participant-provided link or research is an uncompensated contribution and safeguard against appropriation or unfair procurement. Entry should not become mandatory commercial work for Unfire.
14. Distinguish recognition granted by Unfire from formal academic / government / professional accreditation and distinguish competitions Unfire organizes from third-party awards Unfire itself has earned.

No `OPEN` state, live application form, announced cash or supplier-funded prize, final rules or published sponsor relationship until decisions above are verified for the exact edition by an appropriate qualified reviewer/owner and single authorized integrator.
