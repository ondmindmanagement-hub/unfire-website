# Unfire Global Challenges — feasible awards and fair fulfillment (DRAFT)

**STATUS: CONCEPT ONLY. This document does not announce any award or open a contest.** Each reward described below becomes binding only when the owner confirms the fulfillment capacity, exact edition rules, eligibility, tax/consumer implications, and the authorized release integrator approves the public copy. No cash commitment exists.

## Why awards must be valuable and honest

The objective is to give entrants professionally useful recognition and an evidence-based review without requiring travel, calls, videos of themselves, purchases or assignment of their work. Do **not** describe these recognitions as third-party accreditation, guaranteed publicity, recruitment, investment, a purchase order, public funding, or a sponsored prize.

### Founder-deliverable base award — proposed for first two editions

| Proposed reward | Exact deliverable | Delivery condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Challenge distinction** | A dated, non-accredited digital recognition with immutable edition ID, award category, jury decision summary and official publication URL once real and released. | Only a completed, lawfully operated and judged edition; no fictional winner. |
| **Winner's project review** | A concise, structured written report explaining evaluation under the prepublished rubric, key strengths and improvement opportunities. | Explicit review-time quota confirmed before rules publication; never promise all participants personalized feedback without capacity. |
| **Optional creator profile** | A well-edited editorial project page displaying a summary and authorized links. | Separate written permission from entrant for name/logo/visuals/code; entrant can decline publicity without being forced to release IP. |
| **Finalist badge** | Factual badge identifying edition, year, category and finalist status, not a certificate of official university/industry proficiency. | A genuine ranking achieved through the published independent jury; anti-misrepresentation and revocation/correction procedure. |
| **Public learnings** | Aggregate anonymized methodology findings where they can help the community. | No re-identification, no disclosure of unreleased source, performance results or privately submitted datasets. |

### Future donated awards (separate verification)

Software seats, subscriptions, hardware, credits or mentoring **only** if a valid donor gives explicit written permission for the exact number, distribution rights, country eligibility, expiry, support route and conditions. Check any taxes, customs, shipping or reporting obligations. Never copy a vendor's logo or claim sponsorship before authorization. Do not require a live mentoring session; substitute a defined written review only if the donor accepts.

### Future monetary awards

Cash prize -> before publication: verified source and availability of funds; legal organizer/payer; participant country and age restrictions; tax withholding/reporting; disbursement channels and time; complaints for non-delivery; protection against fraud. No debt, deposits or founder-funded promise under the zero-cost rule.

## Pilot-specific possible recognitions

**UGC-001 · AI Reliability & Human Control:** strongest reproducible safe-approval demonstration; strongest failure-mode documentation; strongest accessible explanation. Each category needs a fixed rubric and may be omitted if entries don't meet minimum standards stated in official rules.

**UGC-002 · Audio & Voice Quality:** most intelligible rights-cleared audio workflow; best documented artifacts/measurement; most responsible provenance and consent. No deceptive voice imitation or third-party copyrighted material.

**Important:** Distinctions are **proposed**, not a promise that a winner will exist in any category. Final rules must define whether there is a minimum quality threshold and how a no-award outcome is disclosed consistently; do not create an illusory prize.

## Fulfillment register (PRIVATE, not in a public Git repo)

Each actual obligation must have: edition ID, award code, confirmed provider/payer, authorization reference, award value/type, tax/logistics review, eligibility matrix, documented winner identity through protected process, consent separately for publicity, due date, dispatch date, acknowledgment, issues/remedies, and evidence of delivery. Store personally identifying, financial and correspondence details only in approved restricted systems.

## Human resource gate

For any award promising **personalized written feedback**, estimate time per finalist and confirm reviewers' actual written acceptance before setting a volume cap. Public rules state exactly who gets feedback and by what deadline; publishing the rules creates expectations and potentially enforceable commitments. No automatic assumption that volunteers, university staff or sponsors will perform judging or reviews.

## Sponsor legal caution (JetBrains as example, NOT a partner)

JetBrains' current official [Developer Communities terms](https://www.jetbrains.com/legal/docs/terms/developer-communities-terms/) say benefits such as giveaway subscriptions are discretionary, eligibility is tied to an active community, and certain promotional / data-handling obligations apply. The program does not make Unfire a sponsor beneficiary or establish transferable awards without acceptance and specific authorization. A written eligibility inquiry is not an application or acceptance of program terms.
