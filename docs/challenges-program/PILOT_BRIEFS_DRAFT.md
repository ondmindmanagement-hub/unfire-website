# UGC-001 / UGC-002 — measurable pilot briefs (PRE-LAUNCH CONCEPTS)

**Neither challenge is open.** No applications are being accepted, no jurors have been confirmed, no sponsor has been confirmed and no material or cash prize has been promised. Dates, country eligibility, final legal rules, award capacity and intake remain to be approved.

## UGC-001 — AI Reliability & Human Control

**Public-benefit question:** Can a small AI-agent demonstrator show human-controlled authorization and verifiable outcomes without exposing real users, tokens or systems? This is about a reproducible **synthetic** task, not about access to Unfire's private code or production environment.

**Proposed output:** short written problem statement; architecture diagram or explanation; reproducible inert source/demo or a text-only test transcript; test cases including permission denied, STOP, duplicate task handling, auditable records and failure handling; license/rights declaration and accessible summary. No secret keys or production credentials. No obligation to use Unfire products.

**Proposed rubric (publish only in final official rules):**
- 30% demonstrable correct behavior: clear expected vs actual outcomes and reproducibility.
- 25% human safety: documented stop, privilege boundary, fail-closed actions.
- 25% usefulness: realistic constraints and repeatable applicability.
- 20% communication: clear written explanation, tests and honest limitations.

**Review design:** independent written jury; every finalist receives an auditable scored rubric if resources are confirmed. Synthetic edge cases rather than uncontrolled real systems. Written-only clarification windows. No live demonstrations required.

## UGC-002 — Audio & Voice Quality

**Public-benefit question:** How can creators increase intelligibility, naturalness and technical reliability of original synthetic/synthesized or recorded voices while respecting people's identities and intellectual property?

**Proposed output:** original, rights-cleared 20–60 second reference and processed audio sample (or accessible link), short written signal chain and source/license provenance, technical measurements when appropriate, limitations and reproducible methodology. Provide a written transcript and no live speaking requirement. No cloning of a real person's voice without their documented permission; no misleading celebrity or brand impersonation. Explicit accessibility accommodations for participants who cannot hear.

**Proposed rubric:**
- 30% intelligibility and acoustic usability; explain listening procedure and evidence.
- 25% audible quality: clipping, harsh artifacts and stability, evaluated against published reference.
- 25% provenance, consent, copyright and safety.
- 20% reproducibility, technical documentation and accessibility.

**Review design:** use independently verified audio specialists; blind the review where practicable to avoid brand bias. Listening conditions and formats must be standardized in official rules. A participant may submit audio **files or links** without a video call, on-stage activity or physical presence. Never host copyrighted third-party content without rights.

## Rewards: currently NOT committed

Preferred low-cost benefit is a **specific, truthful written review and optional publication after consent**, but do not promise individual feedback for an unknown number of entries without reviewer capacity. Any donated software license or cash is an **additional edition-specific award** only after written donor authorization, legal/tax review and fulfillment evidence. No implication of professional certification, investment, employment, commercial contract or external endorsement.

## Proposed operational capacity

A trial capacity of up to 30 eligible written submissions per edition, subject to confirmed juror time. If that cap is eventually adopted, it must appear in official rules along with an objective, fair acceptance method (never a random draw or undisclosed preference). Suggested timeline: applications 21–35 days, written Q&A weekly, judges 7–14 days, results after conflict checks; these are planning ranges **not announced dates**.

## Why not launch yet?

Blocking dependencies: organizer's verified legal identity/public disclosures, country rules and tax; frozen official rules with timelines and appeals; independent written judges; verified prize and fulfillment; GDPR rights/privacy; safe entry channel and acknowledgement; QA/security/accessibility; one release integrator. Do not silently set `OPEN` in marketing copy, Kaggle or GitHub Pages.

## Partner-facing value proposition, truthful and nonbinding

Unfire is exploring an international, written-only innovation challenge program. Potential collaborators could contribute an optional short problem statement, an expert judging review, in-kind tools or a funded prize after written agreement. Participation is voluntary, remote and subject to clearly defined scope and conflict safeguards. No named institution is a partner unless it accepts in writing; no sponsor obtains entrants' personal details, exclusive IP or the right to select its preferred winner by payment.
