## Ethical partnering rules
- A possible supporter is **not** a confirmed sponsor, judge, endorsing institution or prize guarantor.
- Sponsors do not receive entrant databases, private submissions or rights without informed, separate and lawful participant authorization.
- Avoid free labor disguised as challenges: publicly valuable problem, bounded work and transparent evaluation.
- No forced vendor lock-in in base categories; provider-specific bonus tracks require separate disclosed rules and real rewards.
- No costs, deposits, in-person obligations, compulsory phone/video or commercial commitments may be accepted.
- After a suitable partner replies, verify its domain and terms and place any agreement behind the legal/release approval gates.
