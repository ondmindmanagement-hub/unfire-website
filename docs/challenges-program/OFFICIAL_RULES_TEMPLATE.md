# Unfire Global Challenges — edition official rules template (NOT APPROVED)

**Internal draft only. No entries may be accepted from this template.** Each bracketed item requires specific evidence, responsible owner approval and country-specific legal review. Do not disclose private home address, identity documents, financial data, applicant data or other secrets in a public GitHub repository.

## 1. Organizer and contact
[Real legal organizer, mandatory public identification, official written contact, governing law]. Do not imply a registered company, license, university partnership, external certification or sponsor without proof.

## 2. Challenge and deliverable
[One independently valuable problem and precise deliverable; objective evaluation method]. Participation is free and remote/asynchronous in writing. **No obligatory calls, videoconferences, public speaking, event attendance, live presentations, travel or purchases.** Optional recordings/audio may be allowed as project deliverables where essential, never live attendance.

## 3. Eligibility
[Specific permitted countries, residence checks and legal basis; adults 18+; individuals/teams; exclusions; conflicts; local restrictions]. Worldwide discoverability does not imply universal eligibility. Unfire employees, affiliated entities, judges and companies under the organizer's control cannot win their own awards.

## 4. Opening, closing and results
[ISO-8601 UTC start, end, judging close, results date and award delivery deadline]. Define outage resolution, withdrawal, reasonable cancellation procedures, precise complaint and appeal windows, and handling of insufficient eligible submissions.

## 5. Written-only entry process
[Approved secure channel, receipt/acknowledgement, required minimal fields, duplicate submissions and evidence limits]. No credentials, malicious executables, confidential client data, nonconsensual voice cloning, unauthorized copyrighted works, vulnerable systems or sensitive personal records. An expression of interest is not a contest entry.

## 6. Human judging and transparency
[At least three verified written judge acceptances, independent majority and conflict recusal], [published rubric weights totaling 100 and anchored scoring descriptors], [selection timetable]. Tie handling: documented independent human reassessment, never chance, voting lottery or arbitrarily changing criteria after launch. AI tools may assist with screening but cannot be the final undisclosed judge.

## 7. Exact rewards, funding, taxes
[Precise type, quantity, value if applicable, funding/donor evidence, fulfillment owner and delivery terms]. Recognition alone must not promise professional credentials, endorsements, employment, pilots or investor access. In-kind awards need transferable rights and actual availability. Cash needs actual ring-fenced/verified funding, payer, fiscal review and safe recipient verification.

## 8. Intellectual property
Entrants retain original work and background IP. Define only the narrow review rights needed for judging. Separate affirmative authorization is required for winner public showcases, use of names/logos, partner introductions or any broader reuse. No blanket transfer or compulsory commercial exploitation.

## 9. Data and privacy
[Actual controller identity and contact, GDPR Article 13 notice, lawful basis per purpose, categories, retention, recipients/processors, international transfers, rights and complaints]. Any marketing must be separate from participation; no automatic marketing opt-in or sale of entrant contact lists. Post-award tax documents use an approved protected channel.

## 10. Fairness, incidents and remedies
[Disqualification standards, plagiarism review, accessibility support, conflicts, handling of technological failures, complaints and reasoned review, lawful limitation of liability]. Include applicable statutory rights without waiving mandatory protections.

## 11. Release and provenance
[Final rules version, frozen SHA-256, exact public HTTPS URL, date, eligible country matrix, verified judges and written acceptance, prize proof, legal owner sign-off, security/QA evidence, one integrator].
