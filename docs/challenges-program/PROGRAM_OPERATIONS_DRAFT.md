## Program goal and non-negotiables

Build one international-facing umbrella, **Unfire Global Challenges**, with many well-scoped skill competitions. Everything must work **asynchronously in writing**: never require travel, conferences, live demo calls, webcam sessions, spoken English, or video attendance. Optional recorded media can be eligible in audio/video-related categories. Participants should receive meaningful review or a verified award; never use them as an unpaid work pool for Unfire. Participation is free, no purchase is required, no random selection or lottery is permitted, participant IP remains theirs, and sponsorship is not a route to manipulate results. **Worldwide discoverability is not worldwide legal eligibility.**

## One edition, one verifiable set of records

Each edition has an immutable ID (e.g. UGC-001), state, exact eligible countries, dates in UTC, versioned official rules and SHA-256, final scoring rubric, judge acceptance and conflict declarations, prize ledger and fulfillment owner, privacy notice, data-retention plan, grievances procedure and a responsible release integrator. Only truthful states are allowed: CONCEPT / PLANNED / OPEN / JUDGING / RESULTS / ARCHIVED / CANCELLED.

- `CONCEPT` and `PLANNED`: no intake, formal entry claim, winner claim, named partnership claim, unfunded prize promise or fake dates.
- `OPEN`: **block by default** until actual evidence shows identity, legal and tax review, country matrix, frozen rules, independent majority of jurors, deliverable prize, approved secure written intake, GDPR handling, incident/appeals plan, accessibility/QA and one release authorization.
- `JUDGING`: intake closed, locked prior criteria; human judges reassess ties (never random selection).
- `RESULTS`: retain human decision record and delivery evidence; obtain separate consent before public winner publicity.
- `ARCHIVED` / `CANCELLED`: transparent historical notices, no open-entry buttons or unsupported claims.

Unfire, employees and affiliates must be **ineligible to win its own organizer awards**. Jury conflicts must be disclosed and conflicted reviewers recused. Automated triage may assist but cannot be the sole adjudicator.

## Awards and sponsor boundaries

1. **Recognition**: truthful digital acknowledgment, reproducible written evaluation and an optional winner showcase only with permission. Never describe this as external accreditation, hiring, funding or investment.
2. **In-kind**: software/hardware/services only if the supplier grants transferable rights, confirms availability, logistics, and any applicable tax/duty.
3. **Cash**: announce only after verified funds, responsible payer, beneficiary eligibility, tax/residency and timely payment process. A sponsor expression of interest is not a financed prize.
4. Sponsors may receive aggregate non-personal insights or finalist introductions **only with the individuals' independent opt-in**; sponsorship never grants default access to their data/IP or a judging advantage.
