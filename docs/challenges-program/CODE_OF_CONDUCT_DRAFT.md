# Unfire Global Challenges — Code of Conduct & written evaluation (DRAFT)

**Not yet in force:** Official competition rules and published organizer identity are not approved. This file proposes safeguards and may be adapted by the legal owner before any application opens.

## Our shared standard
Unfire wants thoughtful, useful and accessible technical/creative work, from people anywhere **they are legally eligible to participate**. Every future edition will be asynchronous, remote and free. No conference, live call, video meeting, live presentation, travel or paid product is required. Technical demos may consist of original audio or recorded media where the category genuinely needs it.

### Respect and inclusivity
- No harassment, intimidation, doxxing, discriminatory conduct, coercive recruitment, threats, impersonation or hostile engagement with other participants/judges.
- Respect individual disability and accessibility needs. Offer equivalent written means to clarify submissions and ask questions; do not penalize accent, camera usage, ability to attend a live event or access to paid hardware.
- No mass messaging, phishing, credential sharing, malware, unauthorized testing of third-party services, malicious automation or exposure of personal/customer data.
- Do not submit work that violates someone else's copyright, trademark, voice/image rights, trade secrets, or consent. Clearly declare substantial AI assistance and license sources, applying the same published rules to everyone.
- Report concerns privately by a designated written organizer contact; no applicant should be required to post a complaint publicly or approach a jury member privately.

### Fair judging
- Publish the problem, all eligibility rules, deadlines in UTC, scoring rubric and award delivery procedure before entries open. Freeze a canonical rules version and maintain a timestamped change record.
- Every judge confirms acceptance, identity, expertise, availability, independence and conflicts **in writing** before review; independently recuse from cases affecting themselves, their employers, customers, relatives or portfolio.
- Sponsors can propose optional topic areas, but cannot pay to predetermine winners or obtain confidential entries.
- Avoid unpublished screening factors and opaque winner selection. AI may assist in formatting/deduplicating submissions but not replace the declared independent human jury.
- For exact ties, the same independent adjudicators perform documented reassessment using published criteria. Never use a lottery or arbitrary unannounced tiebreak.
- Preserve audit decisions privately and share reasoned outcomes/review information according to rules and capacity; allow a bounded written challenge/appeal mechanism.

### Rights and award fulfillment
- Entrants keep their original IP and unrelated background work; grant only necessary evaluation rights. Advertising, public showcase and sponsor introductions require separate informed permission.
- Do not sell applicant data or make participation contingent on marketing permissions.
- Every proposed award must be completely and truthfully specified. A certificate or public feature is recognition **not** an academic/professional accreditation. Written feedback should only be promised if enough reviewer capacity is confirmed.
- In-kind awards require confirmed transferable donor permission and stock/license availability. Cash awards require verified funds, payer identity, tax/delivery obligations and a lawful recipient route before announcement.
- Award winners may decline publicity without forfeiting their award unless a lawful, explicit and fair exception is reviewed and published beforehand.

### Organizer response and due process
- Publish written support and complaint methods in the final terms, with time windows and accessible response standards.
- Investigate reports with proportionality, confidentiality and a chance to respond; record reasons for exclusion and any appeal route. Do not secretly change evaluation weights.
- If cancelled, report the reason and participants' options consistent with applicable law and the official terms. Never make participants work for an unfunded or non-existent reward.

### Prelaunch notice
This is a **proposal**, not an invitation to enter. Contacting Unfire about an edition currently constitutes only an expression of interest. Do not submit confidential information, source repositories containing credentials, ID documents, voice recordings of nonconsenting people or private datasets.
