Editorial standard

The achievement is the result. The evidence is the story.

01 / SOURCE

Verify at the source.

We link directly to the organizer's official result or other primary evidence. A registration or a submission is never presented as a win.

02 / DISTINCTION

Use the exact title.

Winner, finalist, selected participant and independent listing mean different things. We describe each only when the status is substantiated.

03 / CONTEXT

Respect the limits.

Recognition does not by itself establish technical certification, market adoption, investment or institutional endorsement.

For further source-linked facts, engineering references and project disclosures, see Unfire's public record.
