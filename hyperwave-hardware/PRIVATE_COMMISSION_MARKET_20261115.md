## Commercial margin reality
Hypothetical factory/build/ship/quality cost **USD 60,000**, invoice **USD 120,000** -> **USD 60,000 gross contribution (50%)** *only when USD 60k genuinely captures direct fulfillment expense*. **NOT** USD 60k net profit: excludes programme R&D amortisation, taxes, insurance, customer acquisition, legal services, warranty/service/maintenance not already reserved, payment FX and contingencies.

For example, unamortized engineering cost **USD 400,000** needs nearly **7** sales at USD 60k contribution merely to cover that cost (before other fixed costs); one order is not sufficient to finance an entire engineering programme unless contracted design funding explicitly covers it. This arithmetic is illustrative; prior euro R&D estimates cannot be silently substituted for dollar amounts.

The custom $200k–$3m order should be **designed/quoted from a binding work scope**, with validated design and build expenses and contract-defined IP/changes. Do not price based solely on client wealth, and never represent markup as already established.

## Paid advance commissioning: designed, NOT enabled
Stage 0 — **now**: nonbinding introductory inquiry; factually describe the existing digital prototype; request indicative budget and desired personalization; no card processing, deposits or reserve-position guarantees.

Stage 1 — **after technical feasibility**: serious client's private commissioned specification / scope; written design approval, legally identified seller and supplier, credible manufacturing timeline, deposit protections, documented cancellation and inability-to-deliver policy, exports/duties, IP/art rights. Offers and terms reviewed by qualified counsel per buyer jurisdiction.

Stage 2 — **future potential signed commission**: for illustration only, a milestone-based contract could contemplate **50% at contracting, 40% on documented factory acceptance, 10% upon client handover**, subject to escrow/segregated funds and legally valid refund/termination remedies. For USD 120k this means USD 60k / 48k / 12k, but do NOT collect it now and do NOT promise all funds can be spent immediately on production. A bespoke deposit's treatment depends on lawful terms and jurisdiction.

EU Article 16(c) of Consumer Rights Directive excludes *genuinely* customer-specified goods from standard 14-day withdrawal, but that does **not** erase rights for non-delivery/defects or excuse unfair clauses. EU Article 18 provides a default 30-day delivery absent explicit agreement; long manufacturing lead times require a clear agreed date or period and remedies. References:
- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:02011L0083-20260927
- https://europa.eu/youreurope/business/selling-in-eu/selling-goods-services/ecommerce-distance-selling/index_en.htm
- https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/consumers/shopping/returns/index_en.htm

For UAE sellers, Ministry guidance addresses correct seller identity, Arabic-language information for UAE-registered e-commerce sellers, warranty/consumer rights and advertising truth. Extraterritorial scope for a Spain-based seller needs actual legal review before relying on UAE exclusions or exemptions. References:
