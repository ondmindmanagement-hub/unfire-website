- **Do not compromise the identity or physical transformation to fit the former estimate.** Engineer the intended real transforming unit, then quote its complete product cost.
- BOM must explicitly consider movable display lid, deploying keyboard tray/keys, pop-up controls, expandable speaker modules, actuators/manual mechanisms, bearings, hinges, cable chains/flex circuits, interlocks/pinch protection, enclosure rigidity, repairability, quality testing and long-term mechanical cycles. ## Supply-chain route (research, not contracted)
1. Confirm whether the existing audio code can run on an embedded host with acceptable latency, controls, thermal, memory and licensing footprint. Investigate digital-license obligations separately.
2. Choose and engineer the physical enclosure and electronics through measured requirements. “Transforming” physical design must be checked for manufacturability, safety, moving-part pinch hazards, durability and realistic tooling costs. Avoid promising transformation of an actual unit until tested.
3. Prepare a controlled design package (block diagram, interfaces, preliminary BOM, industrial design, tolerances, firmware requirements, assembly/tests). Do not share source code, brand assets, keys or sensitive docs with unvetted suppliers.
4. Ask at least three vetted manufacturers/design-for-manufacture partners for **non-binding quotes** on NRE/design cost, prototype, enclosure, PCBA, certifications, tooling, assembly, test fixtures, MOQ, lead time, storage, freight, warranty and after-sales service.
5. Evaluate **zero-upfront** partnership, royalty or profit-share arrangements only if a supplier explicitly agrees in signed terms. They are not established or assured. Avoid debt or fees without owner approval.
6. Test a **real physical prototype** on camera with end-to-end real sound before making any true-hardware demonstration or acceptance claims.
7. Determine target-country obligations before offering a binding preorder: seller identity, safety/EMC/CE and related compliance as applicable, RoHS/WEEE where applicable, privacy notices, payment terms, cancellation/refund/withdrawal obligations, taxes, fulfillment and warranty. Consumer preorder terms and deadlines require legal review.
8. Only after feasibility, supplier and legal approval should a separate checkout/payment reservation implementation be designed and enabled, with an explicit, realistic delivery window.
