A new instrument language

01 — THE VISION

Not simply played.
Revealed.

Hyperwave 2000 began as an original digital instrument, capable of transforming before the musician's eyes. Its next ambition is radical: to make that sequence physical, tactile and musical through advanced mechatronics. Not a conventional keyboard with an extravagant finish, but a machine whose form is part of the performance.

Explore the authentic 3D concept in its closed and open forms. These are original digital render assets from the working browser experience — not a physical prototype.

The choreography / Four movements

Engineered
to unfold.

Four coordinated transformations define the digital instrument's physical ambition. Motors, sensors and linkages would be engineered to reproduce its movement safely; the original timing and dimensions are not yet mechanically verified.

I.

The awakening

The upper shell and display rise and rotate into position. A proposed precision hinge, motor drive and motion-safety system would manage the opening.

II.

The keys emerge

A 25-note keyboard slides into view, with a staggered arrival of the individual keys in the digital sequence. The physical transmission remains to be designed.

III.

The command surface

The control panel rises and tilts. Nine rotary controls reveal themselves, alongside expressive wheels and a ribbon surface.

IV.

The full expression

Two side-mounted speaker pods extend and articulate on proposed telescopic mechanisms, completing the transformation around the musician.

Reference: inspected Hyperwave 2000 digital animation. A physical device must pass kinematic, acoustic, safety, durability and manufacturer feasibility gates.

Private commissioning / Proposed positioning

Beyond the
ordinary.

We are developing a made-to-order commissioning model for collectors, extraordinary interiors, musical patrons and studios seeking an original technology artefact. The commercial position reflects an ambition for genuine mechanical transformation, specialist engineering and exceptional craftsmanship — not existing stock or completed factory production.

The final quotation, specifications and schedule can only follow validated engineering and manufacturing capacity. No paid reservations are open.
