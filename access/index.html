Unfire / Accessibility research

Technology
for more people.

Unfire Access is an exploratory initiative to make everyday computer interaction more inclusive. We are seeking organisations and researchers who can help evaluate alternative Mac input methods with the people who may benefit from them.

Research-stage initiative · Not a clinically validated or publicly released assistive product

What we are studying

Different ways to use a computer.

Our macOS prototype explores camera-based hand gestures and voice commands as alternatives or complements to conventional input. These modes may not be appropriate or accessible for every person.

Hands

Explore pointer movement, click and scroll interactions using hand tracking, subject to camera conditions and software limitations.

Voice

Explore spoken commands for selected actions, while evaluating recognition errors, accents, noise and user preferences.

Choice and control

Review consent, permissions, stop mechanisms, alternative controls and the ability to fall back to existing assistive technologies.

Collaborative evaluation

A small pilot. Meaningful evidence.

We propose beginning with a remote written scoping discussion and a technical demonstration. Any user study would require an agreed protocol, appropriate safeguarding, informed consent and the partner's required approvals.

01 — Define the real accessibility needIdentify user groups, existing solutions and one specific task worth investigating, with direct input from people with disabilities.
02 — Validate technical readinessAgree a supported Mac configuration, test build, reproducible task, known limitations and an emergency stop or fallback.
03 — Evaluate only with appropriate approvalsMeasure task completion, errors, comfort and user-reported experience. No patient recruitment or clinical claims without the partner's proper oversight.
04 — Share a transparent reportDocument what worked, what failed, possible improvements and whether further development is justified. Publication requires separate permission.

Safety and transparency

Respect comes first.

Current status: Unfire Control is a development preview. Public downloads, medical suitability, clinical outcomes, accessibility compliance and production deployment have not been verified.

Privacy: Initial discussion requires no health records, diagnoses, credentials or identifiable participant data. We do not request sensitive data through ordinary email.

Commercial scope: Preliminary written scoping can be discussed without fees. Any formal development, testing or delivery requires a separate written agreement. Unfire is currently an independent founder-led initiative, not a separately incorporated company.
