02 / Evidence package

Not just a score.

Each engagement aims to answer what was checked, how it was checked, what failed, why it matters and how to reproduce the result.

01. Scope and exclusions

Version, system boundaries, assumptions, authorization and tests explicitly not performed.

02. Reproducible findings

Observed behaviour, expected behaviour, severity, steps and supporting evidence.

03. Prioritised action plan

Clear fixes, estimated relative effort and which decisions still need the system owner.

04. Retest status

What passed, failed, could not be tested or remains unverified. No invented completion claims.

04 / Transparency

Important boundaries.

Are these ISO, ENS or legally mandated audits?

No. These are independent technical reviews within the agreed scope. Unfire is not advertising accredited certification, legal compliance opinions, statutory audit or an official conformity assessment.

Can you inspect our live platform or run penetration tests?

Not by default. No scanning, vulnerability exploitation, production changes or access to private systems without specific written authorization and suitable expertise. Sensitive work may be declined or referred to appropriately qualified specialists.

What proof do you have?

Current Unfire AI evidence is internal and synthetic, not independently validated customer work. Check the existing experimental evidence and public record for source and limitations.

Do you guarantee results or require meetings?

No guaranteed business savings, compliance result or defect-free release. Communication is email-first, remote and written. Enquiries create no commitment; any paid work requires a separate agreement.
