Before you buy.

Is it a subscription?

No. BOLT is a one-time Mac App Store purchase at the price Apple shows for your region. Live AI mode can incur separate charges from OpenAI or Anthropic on your own account.

Will it edit files without asking?

It is designed to show proposed changes and ask for your approval before applying them. Treat the checks as safeguards, not a guarantee, and inspect every edit yourself.

Can I use it without paying for API calls?

Yes, in simulated mode. Live model output needs your own provider credentials and may cost money.

What does it need?

A Mac with macOS 14 or later. For live mode, your own OpenAI and/or Anthropic API access.

Does BOLT collect data?

The developer reports no accounts and no analytics. Live requests go only to the providers you configure, after approval.

Is this the same as a business pilot?

No. The app is a general Mac product. A business feasibility study is a separate, scoped engagement under written agreement. See Unfire for teams.

BOLT supports reviewed AI workflows. It is not an independently certified security product, and no automated check removes every mistake. Back up valuable files and start with projects you can afford to experiment on.
