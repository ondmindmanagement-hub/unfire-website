Cookie Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026 · Scope: website and browser game. App-specific disclosures and store listings also apply.

Cookies are not the only way a website stores information. This notice covers first-party browser storage used for features and the possibility of connections to third-party services. For information on personal data, see the privacy notice.

1. Storage needed for requested functions

Unfire's browser-based experiences may store game progress, audio/graphic preferences, language, local settings, session choices and similar functional information in local storage, session storage, browser cache or service-worker caches. These generally stay on your device until cleared, expired, or replaced by the browser or the application. Clearing them may reset settings and saved progress. The use of technically necessary storage differs from optional tracking technologies.

2. Network services are not all cookies

Loading pages, optional external font resources, browser speech recognition or entering peer-to-peer multiplayer can contact infrastructure outside this website, such as hosting, font, signalling, STUN/TURN or browser-vendor services. A request to a third-party server is not automatically a cookie, but it can involve transmission of network data or provider-controlled storage. The exact services depend on the page and browser; consult the privacy policy and your browser's controls.

3. Analytics and optional technologies

At the date of this notice, the website's planned game-visit analytics system has not been confirmed as deployed. No consent to analytics should be inferred merely from playing, scrolling, or continuing to browse. If non-essential cookies or analytics are enabled, they must first be documented and, when consent is required, users must have an equally accessible way to accept and reject them. A future consent control must allow changing or withdrawing that choice. This page does not claim a specific opt-in panel currently exists if it is not shown on the website.

4. Your controls

You can view and remove cookies and site data through your browser's privacy settings; many browsers also let you limit third-party cookies or use a private window. These settings can affect sign-in, embedded resources, saved game sessions and other optional functions. For questions, or to raise a privacy issue, contact hello@unfire.technology.

5. Changes

We will revise this notice when the actual inventory of technologies, durations, providers or purposes changes. Before activating any optional tracking, the website must show clear information and obtain valid consent where required.
