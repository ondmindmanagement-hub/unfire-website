The idea

Digital reach. Physical presence.

We are exploring a connected entertainment model where creators can reach audiences, players can discover work they value, and stores can become destinations again.

01 / CREATE

Independent publishing

Explore a fair, curated submission path for independent games and selected audiovisual works, with clear rights, technical and editorial checks.

02 / DISCOVER

Digital distribution

Plan a well-organised catalog for eligible releases, clear purchase terms and transparent creator revenue reporting.

03 / COLLECT

Physical editions

Investigate disc, boxed and collectible releases, subject to rights, demand, manufacturing and responsible fulfilment.

04 / CONNECT

A unified library vision

Research a single place to discover compatible titles and connect supported libraries. One interface does not mean every game can run on every device.

05 / EXPERIENCE

Modern entertainment spaces

Explore revitalised gaming stores and video-club-inspired spaces for discovery, demonstrations, retro culture and immersive experiences.

06 / COLLABORATE

Industry-first partnerships

Investigate collaboration with developers, publishers, rights holders, retailers and technology providers where interests align.

For independent creators

A route from original idea to real audience.

We want emerging teams to have options beyond releasing an app or posting a video. Our proposed programme would evaluate eligible projects and explore digital release, physical editions or both.

No submission fees, revenue shares, approval standards or release guarantees have been finalised. We are currently gathering non-confidential feedback and potential collaboration interest.

Proposed publishing review

01 · Rights and identityConfirm the creator's authority to distribute the work and any third-party materials.
02 · Safety and suitabilityReview prohibited content, security, age suitability and applicable consumer safeguards.
03 · Technical qualityAssess usability, stability, supported platforms and build integrity against published criteria.
04 · Clear decisionProvide an approval, specific requested changes or a reasoned rejection with a review path.

Small projects should be judged on merit, not on studio size or production budget. No files or intellectual property should be sent before a separate submission agreement is available.

Future hardware and software

One destination. Honest compatibility.

The longer-term ambition includes a gaming hub and potentially dedicated hardware that make compatible games and collections easier to access.

Compatibility with existing consoles, games and stores requires technical validation and, where applicable, licences, publisher permission or platform agreements. There are no announced integrations with Sony, Microsoft, Nintendo, Valve or other platform owners.

Development priorities

  • Own-platform titles with documented compatibility targets.
  • Supported PC games and authorised distribution methods.
  • Licensed retro releases, ports and lawful preservation approaches.
  • Optional integrations with third-party libraries where permitted.
  • Account security, accessibility, updates and long-term support.
For shops and rights holders

More ways to participate.

We envision opportunities for existing stores rather than claiming to replace them: curated physical games, films, collectibles, repair and customisation concepts, trade-in models and room for new play experiences.

Questions

What is confirmed?

Can I buy the console or games today?

No. This is a concept exploration page. A working console, product pricing and release schedule have not been announced.

Will every existing console game run on Unfire hardware?

That is not a supported claim. Compatibility depends on the individual game, software architecture, hardware, rights and publisher or platform agreements.

Can I submit my project now?

You may email a brief non-confidential expression of interest. A formal submission system, eligibility terms, review criteria and commercial agreements are not yet available.

Does Unfire already work with major gaming brands or retail chains?

No commercial relationship is implied by mentioning these categories. Any future partnership would be announced only after confirmation and permission to disclose it.
