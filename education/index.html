UNFIRE · University research

One company. Multiple research disciplines.

A faculty can select one well-defined academic question, using public or synthetic materials and measurable outcomes. Unfire does not seek unpaid commercial development, student recruitment or a standing university workforce.

01 / RESPONSIBLE AI

Human oversight

Study auditable approval checkpoints, controlled AI workflows and the human cost of intervention using synthetic tasks.

02 / HUMAN-COMPUTER INTERACTION

Inclusive interfaces

Compare gesture and conventional interactions, task accuracy and usability with consent and academic safeguards.

03 / AUDIO & DSP

Sound and measurement

Examine reproducible signal-processing and listening methods using original or suitably licensed audio material.

04 / INTERACTIVE SYSTEMS

Accessible 3D

Measure whether lightweight browser 3D explanations improve understanding compared with equivalent 2D materials.

Research first. Independent evaluation.

Example track · Unfire Control

Technology that supports clearer teaching.

We do not assume technology improves learning on its own. Each use case must be compared with a keyboard, mouse or conventional presentation remote.

01 / Teaching

Presentations and projected whiteboards

Explore slide navigation, selection, scrolling and zoom through gestures and voice, where the specific application supports these commands.

02 / Higher education

Lectures and demonstrations

Test interaction during lectures, project presentations and laboratory demonstrations where presenters need freedom of movement.

03 / Research

Interfaces and accessibility

Study errors, effort, precision, unintended clicks and diverse user needs with researchers. This is not a replacement for validated assistive technology.

No mandatory in-person meetings

Start with a small pilot.

Participation is assessed by email with a responsible institutional contact. We do not request children's data or installation before authorization.

01

Compatibility

Confirm an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 14 or later, camera, microphone, institutional permissions and a connection to a projector or screen.

02

Safe distribution

Verify the version and installation channel, including notarization or official availability as applicable.

03

Guided evaluation

Adult educators, short reversible tasks, keyboard or mouse as alternatives and supervision by the institution's IT team.

04

Results

Measure task completion time, selection errors, false activations, stability and participant feedback.

What remains to be demonstrated

Accuracy and latency under varying classroom lighting; hardware compatibility; voice recognition robustness; the impact of background noise; privacy and data retention; and acceptance by institutional IT administrators. Eye tracking is still experimental and is excluded from the base pilot.

Transparency and student protection

What an institution needs.

Initial requirements

  • Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 14+. The current arm64 build is not designed for Intel Macs.
  • Available camera and microphone; accessibility and system permissions approved by institutional IT.
  • A connected external monitor, presentation screen or projector. The whiteboard does not run Unfire Control by itself.
  • An adult teacher or staff member participating with consent; start with fictional, non-sensitive material.
  • A reviewed distribution method, without asking anyone to disable Gatekeeper or institutional security policies.

Other Unfire experiences

In addition to Control, we can explore educational proposals involving interactive 3D and creative environments, audio production and processing, AI workflow oversight and energy data visualization. Every product has its own maturity level and requirements; they are not automatically delivered as a bundled installation.

Licensing and collaboration

A pilot does not grant redistribution rights, access to source code or unlimited institutional use. Any paid pilot, integration, license or support would require written terms and a review of applicable rights and obligations.

For universities · schools · research centres · laboratories

Which question should we test?

Send the proposed faculty or course area, one research question and the appropriate institutional contact. For an Unfire Control presentation study, specify the Mac, macOS and screen setup as well. Do not send student records, credentials or confidential code. We respond by email.
