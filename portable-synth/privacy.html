Portable Synth — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026 · Applies to Portable Synth for iPhone and iPad (bundle identifier technology.unfire.synth).

This notice describes how the Portable Synth app works with audio, camera input, imported media, and locally stored projects. It is separate from the Unfire website and Y2K Robots game privacy notices.

1. No account or advertising tracking

Portable Synth can be played and used without registering an account. The current app does not include an advertising network, in-app analytics SDK, or a Unfire service that uploads your music projects or captured camera footage. We do not sell user data.

2. Audio and local projects

Synthesis, sequencing, and instrument effects run on your device. Recordings, song arrangements, sound settings, and other projects are kept in the app's local storage until you delete them, export them, or remove the app. If you initiate an export or share, the destination you select handles that copy under its own terms. Device backups may include app data depending on your Apple device and backup settings.

3. Microphone

Microphone access is optional and requested when you choose a feature that records audio or voice. Audio is used to create a local recording. Portable Synth does not automatically send microphone recordings to Unfire. You can change microphone permission at any time in iOS Settings.

4. Camera and gesture control

If you enable optional hand or fingertip control for effects, the app may request camera access. Camera frames are processed on the device to determine control positions; the feature is not designed to save camera video or upload it to Unfire. You can stop the feature and revoke camera permission in iOS Settings.

5. Imported photos, video, and audio

You can choose media to import for musical or visual features. The app uses the selected files for those features on your device. It does not automatically transfer your selected media to an Unfire server. The system photo picker, file providers, and any share or export destination may have their own privacy practices.

6. Support messages and Apple services

If you email support, we receive the contact information and content you choose to provide and use them to answer your request. Our email provider processes these messages for delivery. Apple independently operates App Store distribution, permission prompts, optional device backups, and any diagnostic reports you choose to share with Apple, subject to Apple's policies.

7. Your controls and deletion

You can manage permissions in iOS Settings, remove projects in the app where supported, and delete app data by removing Portable Synth. Copies you exported, shared, or backed up may need to be removed separately from their destinations. Contact us with privacy questions or support requests.

8. Updates and contact

If a later version adds accounts, online uploads, analytics, or materially different data practices, we will update this notice and the corresponding App Store disclosures. For privacy questions, contact hello@unfire.technology.
