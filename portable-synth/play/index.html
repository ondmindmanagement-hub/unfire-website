Tap, click or hold a key to play. On a desktop keyboard, use A W S E D F T G Y H U J from C to B. Audio starts only after your interaction. On smaller screens, scroll sideways across the keyboard to reach every note.

Notes are synthesized on your own device. The sound engine does not transmit or record audio. The page loads its static assets from this website. This keyboard page does not include recording or WAV export. Our separate browser step sequencer lets you compose patterns and export experimental WAV audio; neither browser tool is the full Android application.

Beyond this experiment

Android and browser editions are different.

The full Portable Synth Daw Android Direct edition is available from the official Unfire product page. Google Play access is a separate closed-testing channel. This browser experiment does not reproduce the full Android application.
