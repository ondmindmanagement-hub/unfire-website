i
A browser experiment, not the full Android DAW

This self-contained step sequencer is distinct from the existing Portable Synth browser keyboard and the native Android Portable Synth Daw. It does not offer multitrack recording, effects chains, MIDI controller support or native format compatibility. Audio playback needs a browser with Web Audio; WAV export additionally needs OfflineAudioContext. Sound levels and export formats should be checked in your browser before release.
