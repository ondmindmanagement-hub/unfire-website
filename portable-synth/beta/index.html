The 14-day commitment

Stay enrolled. Test sincerely.

Why 14 days?

Google Play's closed-testing requirement involves at least 12 real testers staying opted in for 14 continuous days before we can request production access. We'd appreciate your staying opted in for at least 14 days, ideally 16, and trying the app on multiple occasions. Approval isn't automatic.

Where to report issues

Email hello@unfire.technology with your Android version, device model, steps to reproduce and any useful observations. Do not send passwords, private files or sensitive personal data. We value genuine feedback, including criticism.

This page has no signup form, third-party analytics scripts or payment collection. Google Play and Google Groups are separate services governed by their own terms and privacy policies.
