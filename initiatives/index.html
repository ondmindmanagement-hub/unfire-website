Working principles

Clarity before claims.

Evidence: planned work, internal prototypes, published listings, independently validated outcomes and delivered commercial contracts are distinct statuses.

Safety: no sensitive participant data, production credentials, or live operational access in an initial inquiry.

Contact: written, remote and asynchronous by email. Unfire is not a separately incorporated company at this stage.
